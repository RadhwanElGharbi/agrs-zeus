  - Special surcharge: 15-25% for Natura 2000 sites
  - High-value agricultural land (truffle areas): Up to 300% premium

## Strategic Regulatory Considerations

### Opportunities
1. **DL Energia 2025 Fast-Tracking:** Project may qualify for expedited permitting (18-24 months vs. 24-36 months) if positioned as enhancing "system flexibility" and energy security
2. **SNAM Partnership:** TSO actively seeking infrastructure partnerships amid flow pattern changes
3. **EU Funding:** Projects supporting Italy's climate targets may qualify for REPowerEU funds, especially if designed for future hydrogen blending capability

### Risks
1. **EU Scrutiny:** PSV-TTF spread intervention by Italian government may trigger EU Commission review of all Italian gas infrastructure projects
2. **Retroactive Tariff Adjustments:** DL Energia 2025 may introduce changes affecting project economics
3. **Market Distortion Concerns:** Project must be positioned to avoid perception of artificial market manipulation
4. **Community Opposition:** Recent precedent shows 18+ month delays due to local environmental concerns (2024 SNAM Cascia project)

### Mitigation Strategies
- Frame project as enhancing local energy security and system flexibility (not corporate profit)
- Demonstrate contribution to 2030 climate targets without undermining decarbonization goals
- Incorporate design considerations for future hydrogen blending (even if not immediate)
- Establish proactive community engagement program with local economic development components
- Position as complementing (not competing with) renewable energy transition
