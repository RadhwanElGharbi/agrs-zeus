# Qatar GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### National LiDAR-Derived DEM
**Dataset name:** Qatar National DEM from Aerial LiDAR
**Source/provider:** Center for GIS (CGIS) - Ministry of Municipality
**Resolution/scale:** 5m resolution
**Temporal coverage:** 2022 (aerial survey completed March-April 2022)
**Update frequency:** Periodic aerial surveys (system operational since 2022)
**Access type:** Restricted/requires formal request from government agencies
**API availability:** No public API
**Official URL:** https://www.gisqatar.org.qa/en/
**Quality assessment:** High quality - generated from manned aircraft LiDAR surveys with 20cm aerial photography accuracy. CGIS operates a dual-engine aircraft with specialized remote sensing equipment. The DEM covers entire Qatar including Halul Island.[1][2][3][4]

### Copernicus DEM GLO-30
**Dataset name:** Copernicus Digital Elevation Model GLO-30
**Source/provider:** European Space Agency (ESA) / Copernicus Programme
**Resolution/scale:** 30m resolution (1 arc-second)
**Temporal coverage:** 2010-2015 (TanDEM-X Mission data)
**Update frequency:** Static product with periodic updates (2023_1 version available)
**Access type:** Open/free
**API availability:** Yes - Google Earth Engine API, Copernicus Data Space API
**Official URL:** https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM
**Quality assessment:** Excellent global coverage with vertical accuracy RMSE of 4.89m, outperforming SRTM and ASTER. Digital Surface Model includes buildings and vegetation. Best freely available global DEM for Qatar.[5][6][7][8]

### Copernicus DEM GLO-90
**Dataset name:** Copernicus Digital Elevation Model GLO-90
**Source/provider:** European Space Agency (ESA)
**Resolution/scale:** 90m resolution (3 arc-seconds)
**Temporal coverage:** 2010-2015
**Update frequency:** Static
**Access type:** Open/free
**API availability:** Yes - same as GLO-30
**Official URL:** Same as GLO-30
**Quality assessment:** Lower resolution version suitable for regional overview.[6]

## 2. LAND COVER / LAND USE

### ESA WorldCover 2021
**Dataset name:** ESA WorldCover 10m v200
**Source/provider:** European Space Agency (ESA) - VITO Remote Sensing consortium
**Resolution/scale:** 10m resolution
**Temporal coverage:** 2021 (v200), 2020 (v100 also available)
**Update frequency:** Annual updates
**Access type:** Open/free (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0)
**API availability:** Yes - Google Earth Engine API
**Official URL:** https://worldcover2021.esa.int
**Quality assessment:** Highest resolution global land cover product with 77% overall accuracy. 11 land cover classes including built-up, cropland, bare/sparse vegetation, and water bodies. Based on Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 data. Excellent for Qatar's arid environment.[9][10][11][12]

### Qatar National Land Use Data
**Dataset name:** Qatar Topographic Database
**Source/provider:** Center for GIS (CGIS) - Ministry of Municipality
**Resolution/scale:** High resolution (derived from 20cm aerial imagery)
**Temporal coverage:** Current (updated July 2022)
**Update frequency:** Regular updates through aerial surveys
**Access type:** Restricted/requires request
**API availability:** No public API
**Official URL:** https://www.gisqatar.org.qa/en/
**Quality assessment:** Highest resolution land use data for Qatar, maintained as national topographic database.[13][1]

## 3. HYDROLOGY / WATER

### National Water Resources Data
**Dataset name:** Qatar Groundwater and Water Resources Database
**Source/provider:** Ministry of Municipality / Kahramaa (Qatar Water & Electricity Corp)
**Resolution/scale:** Well locations and aquifer mapping
**Temporal coverage:** Current (monitoring ongoing)
**Update frequency:** Continuous monitoring
**Access type:** Restricted/government agencies only
**API availability:** No
**Official URL:** Limited public access
**Quality assessment:** Comprehensive data on Qatar's two main aquifers (Rus and Abu Samra), groundwater wells (292 managed by Kahramaa), and treated sewage effluent injection sites. Critical for pipeline routing to avoid water infrastructure.[14][15]

### Global Surface Water Explorer
**Dataset name:** JRC Global Surface Water Mapping
**Source/provider:** European Commission Joint Research Centre
**Resolution/scale:** 30m resolution
**Temporal coverage:** 1984-present (38+ years)
**Update frequency:** Annual updates
**Access type:** Open/free
**API availability:** Yes - Google Earth Engine API
**Official URL:** http://global-surface-water.appspot.com
**Quality assessment:** Comprehensive temporal mapping of surface water extent, occurrence, and change. Valuable for Qatar despite limited surface water, particularly for coastal areas and wadis during rainfall events.[16]

### HydroSHEDS
**Dataset name:** Hydrological data and maps based on Shuttle Elevation Derivatives at multiple Scales
**Source/provider:** USGS / Conservation International / WWF
**Resolution/scale:** 3 arc-seconds (~90m)
**Temporal coverage:** Derived from SRTM (2000)
**Update frequency:** HydroSHEDS v2 in development using TanDEM-X
**Access type:** Open/free
**API availability:** Direct download, some integration with Earth Engine
**Official URL:** https://www.hydrosheds.org
**Quality assessment:** Standard global hydrographic baseline. Limited utility for Qatar due to ephemeral wadi systems, but useful for flow accumulation and drainage direction mapping.[17][18][19]

## 4. INFRASTRUCTURE - ROADS

### National Road Network
**Dataset name:** Qatar Road Network Database (Flowline Dataset)
**Source/provider:** Ashghal (Public Works Authority)
**Resolution/scale:** High resolution carriageway centerline representation
**Temporal coverage:** Current (covers 14,500 lane-km network)
**Update frequency:** Continuous updates
**Access type:** Restricted/requires formal request
**API availability:** No public API
**Official URL:** https://www.ashghal.gov.qa/en/
**Quality assessment:** Most comprehensive and accurate road network dataset for Qatar. Includes complete road hierarchy, asset management data, Road Asset Management System (RAMS), Linear Referencing System (LRS). Pavement inspection completed for entire network. Essential for pipeline corridor planning.[20][21][22][23]

### Qatar Open Data Roads
**Dataset name:** Road Infrastructure Components
**Source/provider:** Planning and Statistics Authority / data.gov.qa
**Resolution/scale:** Statistical aggregations
**Temporal coverage:** Annual statistics
**Update frequency:** Annual
**Access type:** Open/free
**API availability:** Yes - OpenDataSoft API (v2.1)
**Official URL:** https://www.data.gov.qa/explore/
**Quality assessment:** Provides statistical overview but lacks geometric detail. API available at https://www.data.gov.qa/api/explore/v2.1.[24][25][26][27][28]

### OpenStreetMap Qatar
**Dataset name:** OpenStreetMap Qatar Road Network
**Source/provider:** OpenStreetMap Community
**Resolution/scale:** Variable quality
**Temporal coverage:** Current (continuously updated)
**Update frequency:** Real-time community updates
**Access type:** Open/free (ODbL license)
**API availability:** Yes - Overpass API
**Official URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org
**Quality assessment:** Moderate to good completeness for major roads in Qatar. OSM road data quality in Gulf region generally acceptable for main highways but may lack detailed secondary roads. Requires validation against official sources. Studies show OSM completeness varies significantly by region.[29][30][31]

## 7. INFRASTRUCTURE - PIPELINES

### QatarEnergy Pipeline Network
**Dataset name:** QatarEnergy Pipeline Integrity Management System
**Source/provider:** QatarEnergy (formerly Qatar Petroleum)
**Resolution/scale:** Engineering-grade GIS with CGIS-QNG95 coordinate system
**Temporal coverage:** Current (historical data since operations began)
**Update frequency:** Real-time integrity monitoring
**Access type:** Highly restricted/internal only
**API availability:** No external access
**Official URL:** Internal corporate systems
**Quality assessment:** Comprehensive pipeline network data including Dukhan Field operations, Ras Laffan facilities, Mesaieed port connections, offshore production facilities. Includes integrity management data, corridor cross-sections, as-built drawings. Access only through QatarEnergy coordination for authorized projects. Pipeline corridors follow strict specifications documented in QP guidelines.[32][33][34]

### National Pipeline Registry
**Dataset name:** Qatar Oil & Gas Infrastructure Registry
**Source/provider:** Ministry of Municipality / Ministry of Energy
**Resolution/scale:** Varies
**Temporal coverage:** Current
**Update frequency:** As-built submissions required
**Access type:** Restricted/government and authorized operators only
**API availability:** No
**Official URL:** Accessed through formal government channels
**Quality assessment:** Regulatory database of all oil and gas pipelines in Qatar. Essential for conflict checking during route planning.

### OpenStreetMap Pipelines Qatar
**Dataset name:** OSM Qatar Pipeline Infrastructure
**Source/provider:** OpenStreetMap Community
**Resolution/scale:** Very limited
**Temporal coverage:** Current
**Update frequency:** Minimal
**Access type:** Open/free
**API availability:** Yes - Overpass API
**Official URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org
**Quality assessment:** Extremely incomplete and unreliable. OSM pipelines globally are typically incomplete due to security sensitivities. Not suitable for pipeline routing analysis.[35][36]

## 8. PROTECTED AREAS / ENVIRONMENTAL

### National Protected Areas
**Dataset name:** Qatar Protected Areas and Nature Reserves
**Source/provider:** Ministry of Environment and Climate Change (MECC) - Office for State Protected Areas
**Resolution/scale:** Polygon boundaries for protected areas
**Temporal coverage:** Current
**Update frequency:** As new areas designated
**Access type:** Restricted/requires request, some data via MECC
**API availability:** No
**Official URL:** https://www.mecc.gov.qa/
**Quality assessment:** Qatar has 14 protected areas including Shahaniya Wildlife Park, Ras Osheirij Protected Area, Al Mas-habiyya Protected Area, and Al Reem UNESCO-MAB Biosphere Reserve. Covers 15.8% terrestrial (1,804.7 km²) and 2.3% marine (733.2 km²). National strategy targets 17% terrestrial coverage. Essential for pipeline routing to avoid conservation areas.[37][38][39][40]

### World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA)
**Dataset name:** WDPA Qatar Coverage
**Source/provider:** UNEP-WCMC / IUCN
**Resolution/scale:** Vector polygons
**Temporal coverage:** Updated September 2024
**Update frequency:** Monthly updates
**Access type:** Open/free (registered users)
**API availability:** Yes - Protected Planet API
**Official URL:** https://www.protectedplanet.net/country/QAT
**Quality assessment:** Contains 14 protected areas for Qatar (excluding 1 UNESCO-MAB Biosphere Reserve from statistics). Coverage reported as 15.8% terrestrial and 2.27% marine. Good global standard but may lag national data. Note: Management effectiveness evaluations not reported (0% coverage).[41][42][43][44]

## 10. CLIMATE / WEATHER

### Qatar Meteorology Department Data
**Dataset name:** Qatar Climate and Weather Database
**Source/provider:** Qatar Meteorology Department (Civil Aviation Authority)
**Resolution/scale:** Station-based observations
**Temporal coverage:** Historical archive to current
**Update frequency:** Real-time observations, historical archives
**Access type:** Limited public access, requires request for detailed data
**API availability:** No public API (Q Weather mobile app available)
**Official URL:** https://www.qweather.gov.qa/ (https://caa.gov.qa/en/meteorology)
**Quality assessment:** Comprehensive weather station network providing temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind data. Essential for pipeline design parameters. Historical climate data available through formal requests. Annual data summaries published on data.gov.qa.[45][46][47][48][49][50]

### ERA5 Reanalysis
**Dataset name:** ERA5 Global Climate Reanalysis
**Source/provider:** European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) / Copernicus Climate Change Service
**Resolution/scale:** ~30km resolution (0.25° x 0.25°)
**Temporal coverage:** 1940-present
**Update frequency:** Continuous (3-month lag)
**Access type:** Open/free (registration required)
**API availability:** Yes - CDS API
**Official URL:** https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu
**Quality assessment:** Highest quality global reanalysis dataset. Provides hourly atmospheric data including temperature, wind, precipitation. Excellent for Qatar climate baseline and extreme weather analysis for pipeline design.

### WorldClim 2.1
**Dataset name:** WorldClim Global Climate Data
**Source/provider:** WorldClim Project
**Resolution/scale:** 30 arc-seconds (~1km) to 10 arc-minutes
**Temporal coverage:** 1970-2000 baseline, future scenarios available
**Update frequency:** Periodic major updates
**Access type:** Open/free
**API availability:** Direct download, some integration with R packages
**Official URL:** https://www.worldclim.org
**Quality assessment:** Standard climatology dataset for temperature, precipitation, solar radiation, wind. Good for baseline climate characterization of pipeline routes.

## 12. POPULATION / DEMOGRAPHICS

### National Census Data
**Dataset name:** Qatar Census 2020 Results with Spatial Data
**Source/provider:** Planning and Statistics Authority (PSA)
**Resolution/scale:** Municipality, zone, and district levels (781 districts)
**Temporal coverage:** Census years (2010, 2015, 2020)
**Update frequency:** Decennial census with interim estimates
**Access type:** Open/free via Qatar Atlas and data.gov.qa
**API availability:** Yes - data.gov.qa API
**Official URL:** https://gis.psa.gov.qa/QatarAtlas/
**Quality assessment:** Official census data with comprehensive demographic statistics. Qatar Atlas provides interactive mapping and analysis tools. Population density, age composition, nationality breakdowns available at fine spatial resolution. Essential for assessing population exposure along pipeline corridors.[54][55][13][51]

### WorldPop Qatar
**Dataset name:** WorldPop Qatar Population Density
**Source/provider:** WorldPop Project / University of Southampton
**Resolution/scale:** 100m resolution (~3 arc-seconds) gridded dataset
**Temporal coverage:** 2000-2020 annual datasets
**Update frequency:** Annual (historical), periodic updates
**Access type:** Open/free (Creative Commons)
**API availability:** Direct download from WorldPop hub
**Official URL:** https://hub.worldpop.org/ and https://data.humdata.org/dataset/worldpop-population-density-for-qatar
**Quality assessment:** High-resolution gridded population estimates. Provides continuous surface rather than administrative unit aggregations. Useful for pipeline route analysis to minimize population exposure. UN-adjusted versions available. ~1km density datasets also available.[56][57][58][59][54]

### LandScan Global Population
**Dataset name:** LandScan Global Population Database
**Source/provider:** Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL)
**Resolution/scale:** ~1km resolution (30 arc-seconds)
**Temporal coverage:** Annual datasets (2000-present)
**Update frequency:** Annual
**Access type:** Restricted/requires registration and approval
**API availability:** No
**Official URL:** https://landscan.ornl.gov
**Quality assessment:** Ambient population distribution (24-hour average). Higher spatial resolution modeling than census data alone. Requires registration for access but widely used for infrastructure risk assessment.

## 13. SATELLITE IMAGERY (COMMERCIAL & FREE)

### Maxar Technologies
**Dataset name:** WorldView Legion, WorldView-3, WorldView-2, GeoEye-1
**Source/provider:** Maxar Technologies
**Resolution/scale:** 30cm (0.3m) to 50cm panchromatic, 1.2-2m multispectral
**Temporal coverage:** Extensive archive + daily revisit capability
**Update frequency:** On-demand tasking or archive access
**Access type:** Commercial/purchase (varies by license type)
**API availability:** Yes - Maxar platforms, SecureWatch, MGP Pro subscription
**Official URL:** https://www.maxar.com
**Quality assessment:** Highest resolution commercial imagery available. Excellent archive coverage of Qatar. Pricing: Archive $20-30/km² (min 25 km²), New tasking $30-80/km² depending on priority. Stereo pairs available for 3D modeling ($60-90/km²). Essential for detailed pipeline route surveying and as-built verification.[60][61][62][63][64]

### Airbus Intelligence
**Dataset name:** Pléiades Neo, Pléiades, SPOT 6/7
**Source/provider:** Airbus Defence and Space
**Resolution/scale:** 30cm (Pléiades Neo) to 1.5m (SPOT)
**Temporal coverage:** Extensive archive + 1-day revisit (Pléiades Neo constellation)
**Update frequency:** On-demand tasking
**Access type:** Commercial/purchase
**API availability:** Yes - OneAtlas platform with API access
**Official URL:** https://space-solutions.airbus.com / https://oneatlas.airbus.com
**Quality assessment:** Excellent coverage of Qatar with very high resolution. Competitive alternative to Maxar. OneAtlas subscription starts ~€1,200/year for streaming access. Similar pricing to Maxar for tasking. Stereo capability available.[62][65][66][60]

### Planet Labs
**Dataset name:** SkySat (high-res) and PlanetScope (medium-res)
**Source/provider:** Planet Labs
**Resolution/scale:** SkySat: 50cm, PlanetScope: 3-5m
**Temporal coverage:** Daily global coverage (PlanetScope), SkySat on-demand
**Update frequency:** Daily
**Access type:** Commercial/subscription or purchase
**API availability:** Yes - Planet API
**Official URL:** https://www.planet.com
**Quality assessment:** Unique value proposition with daily 3-5m coverage enabling change detection. SkySat provides sub-meter resolution. Good for monitoring pipeline construction progress and detecting encroachments along corridors.

### Sentinel-2 (Free Baseline)
**Dataset name:** Sentinel-2 MSI Level-2A
**Source/provider:** European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme
**Resolution/scale:** 10m (visible bands), 20m (red edge/SWIR), 60m (atmospheric)
**Temporal coverage:** 2015-present
**Update frequency:** 5-day revisit (2 satellites)
**Access type:** Open/free
**API availability:** Yes - Copernicus Data Space API, Google Earth Engine, AWS
**Official URL:** https://dataspace.copernicus.eu
**Quality assessment:** Best free multispectral imagery. 10m resolution excellent for regional analysis and monitoring. Atmospheric correction applied (Level-2A). Ideal for land cover mapping, vegetation monitoring, and change detection along pipeline routes. Essential baseline imagery.

## KEY RECOMMENDATIONS

**Priority National Sources:** For pipeline routing in Qatar, prioritize official data from:
1. **CGIS** (Ministry of Municipality) - 5m LiDAR DEM, 20cm aerial imagery, topographic database
2. **Ashghal** - Road network, infrastructure corridors, utility coordination
3. **QatarEnergy** - Existing pipeline networks and corridor standards
4. **Planning and Statistics Authority** - Administrative boundaries, demographics, Qatar Atlas
5. **MECC** - Protected areas and environmental constraints

**Best Global Datasets:** When national data unavailable:
1. **Copernicus DEM GLO-30** (30m) - best free elevation data
2. **ESA WorldCover 2021** (10m) - best land cover classification
3. **Sentinel-2** (10m) - best free baseline imagery
4. **ERA5** - comprehensive climate data
5. **WDPA** - protected areas baseline

**Commercial Imagery:** Budget $20-40/km² for archive imagery from Maxar or Airbus for detailed route surveying. Consider Planet subscription for monitoring.

**Critical Access Requirements:** Most high-quality Qatar datasets require formal requests through government channels. Plan for 2-6 month lead time for official data acquisition through ministry coordination.
