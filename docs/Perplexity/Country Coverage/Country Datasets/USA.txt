# Comprehensive GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing in the United States

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) - LiDAR-Derived DEMs
**Dataset Name:** 3DEP Standard Digital Elevation Models[1][2]
**Source/Provider:** U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)[1]
**Resolution/Scale:** 1-meter (project-based LiDAR), 1/3 arc-second (~10m), 1 arc-second (~30m)[3]
**Temporal Coverage:** 2000-present, continuously updated[4]
**Update Frequency:** Continuous integration as new LiDAR data becomes available[3]
**Access Type:** Open/Free[2]
**API Availability:** Yes - The National Map API (TNMAccess), OpenTopography API, Google Earth Engine[5][6][7]
**Official URL:** https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program[1]
**Quality Assessment:** Highest quality national elevation data for the US. The 1m LiDAR-derived DEMs represent bare-earth topography with vertical accuracy typically better than 10cm RMSE for flat terrain. Seamless within projects but may have slight discontinuities across project boundaries. Covers CONUS, Alaska, Hawaii, and territories with varying completeness.[2][3]

### Copernicus DEM (Global Coverage)
**Dataset Name:** Copernicus Digital Elevation Model  
**Source/Provider:** European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme  
**Resolution/Scale:** 30m and 90m  
**Temporal Coverage:** 2011-2015 (acquisition period)  
**Update Frequency:** Static (completed mission)  
**Access Type:** Open/Free  
**API Availability:** Yes - via OpenTopography and various portals  
**Official URL:** Available through multiple distributors  
**Quality Assessment:** Good global coverage with consistent quality. For US applications, 3DEP is superior, but Copernicus provides seamless coverage including areas not yet covered by 3DEP LiDAR.

## 2. LAND COVER / LAND USE

### National Land Cover Database (NLCD)
**Dataset Name:** NLCD 2021 and Annual NLCD[8][9]
**Source/Provider:** Multi-Resolution Land Characteristics (MRLC) Consortium - USGS/EPA/NOAA/USFS partnership[9][8]
**Resolution/Scale:** 30m (Landsat-based)[10]
**Temporal Coverage:** Historical epochs (2001, 2004, 2006, 2008, 2011, 2013, 2016, 2019, 2021) plus annual products from 2000-present[11]
**Update Frequency:** Major releases every 2-5 years; annual products updated yearly[11]
**Access Type:** Open/Free[8]
**API Availability:** Yes - WMS services available through MRLC[8]
**Official URL:** https://www.mrlc.gov[8]
**Quality Assessment:** Authoritative US land cover product with 16 Anderson Level II classes. Excellent temporal consistency for change detection. Overall accuracy typically 85-90%. Includes impervious surface, tree canopy, and land cover change products. Essential for environmental impact assessments.[12][9]

### ESA WorldCover (Global)
**Dataset Name:** ESA WorldCover  
**Source/Provider:** European Space Agency  
**Resolution/Scale:** 10m (Sentinel-based)  
**Temporal Coverage:** 2020, 2021  
**Update Frequency:** Annual  
**Access Type:** Open/Free  
**API Availability:** Yes - WMS/WFS services  
**Official URL:** Available through ESA portals  
**Quality Assessment:** Higher resolution than NLCD but with simplified classification scheme (11 classes). Excellent for fine-scale land cover analysis.

## 3. HYDROLOGY / WATER

### NHDPlus High Resolution (NHDPlus HR)
**Dataset Name:** National Hydrography Dataset Plus High Resolution[13][14]
**Source/Provider:** U.S. Geological Survey / U.S. EPA[14][13]
**Resolution/Scale:** 1:24,000 or better[13]
**Temporal Coverage:** Current - based on latest NHD updates[15]
**Update Frequency:** Continuous updates through 3D Hydrography Program (3DHP)[15]
**Access Type:** Open/Free[14]
**API Availability:** Yes - The National Map API, WMS/WFS services[15]
**Official URL:** https://www.usgs.gov/national-hydrography[13]
**Quality Assessment:** Most comprehensive hydrography dataset for the US. Includes streams, rivers, lakes, wetlands, flow direction, catchments, and value-added attributes. Complete coverage for CONUS, Hawaii, US Territories, with Alaska in progress. Derived from 10m 3DEP data ensuring high accuracy. Critical for water crossing analysis and watershed delineation.[14][13]

### NHDPlus Version 2 (Legacy)
**Dataset Name:** NHDPlus V2  
**Source/Provider:** EPA/USGS[14]
**Resolution/Scale:** 1:100,000  
**Temporal Coverage:** Complete national coverage  
**Update Frequency:** Static (superseded by NHDPlus HR)[14]
**Access Type:** Open/Free  
**API Availability:** Limited  
**Official URL:** https://www.epa.gov/waterdata/nhdplus-national-hydrography-dataset-plus[14]
**Quality Assessment:** Complete national coverage with flow routing and catchment delineation. Still useful for network analysis where NHDPlus HR coverage is incomplete.

## 4. INFRASTRUCTURE - ROADS

### National Transportation Atlas Database (NTAD) - Road Networks
**Dataset Name:** NTAD Roads and Highways[16][17]
**Source/Provider:** Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS), U.S. Department of Transportation[16]
**Resolution/Scale:** Varies by dataset; typically 1:100,000 or better  
**Temporal Coverage:** Current[16]
**Update Frequency:** Continuous updates (changed from annual in 2016)[16]
**Access Type:** Open/Free[16]
**API Availability:** Yes - REST API via ArcGIS services[17][18]
**Official URL:** https://www.bts.gov/ntad[16]
**Quality Assessment:** Comprehensive national road network including functional classification, ownership, and attributes. Approximately 90 datasets in total. Suitable for national and regional planning. API access enables programmatic queries.[18][16]

### Census TIGER/Line Roads
**Dataset Name:** TIGER/Line Shapefiles - Roads[19]
**Source/Provider:** U.S. Census Bureau[20]
**Resolution/Scale:** High detail, typically 1:24,000 equivalent  
**Temporal Coverage:** Current (as of January 1 of release year)[19]
**Update Frequency:** Annual updates (September release)[19]
**Access Type:** Open/Free[20]
**API Availability:** Yes - TIGERweb GeoServices REST API[21]
**Official URL:** https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html[19]
**Quality Assessment:** Most detailed free road network for the US. Includes all public roads with detailed attribution. Excellent topology. Updated annually but may lag behind commercial providers for very recent changes.[22][20]

### OpenStreetMap (OSM) US Coverage
**Dataset Name:** OpenStreetMap United States  
**Source/Provider:** OpenStreetMap Community  
**Resolution/Scale:** Variable, typically very detailed in urban areas  
**Temporal Coverage:** Current (live updates)  
**Update Frequency:** Real-time continuous updates  
**Access Type:** Open/Free (ODbL license)  
**API Availability:** Yes - Overpass API, various extracts  
**Official URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org  
**Quality Assessment:** Excellent coverage in urban areas, variable in rural regions. Generally very good road network completeness in the US (>95% of major roads). Crowdsourced data requires validation but often more current than official sources. Road attributes vary by region.

## 7. INFRASTRUCTURE - PIPELINES

### National Pipeline Mapping System (NPMS)
**Dataset Name:** NPMS Pipeline Data[28][29]
**Source/Provider:** Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA), U.S. Department of Transportation[29][28]
**Resolution/Scale:** Transmission pipelines only (not gathering or distribution)[28]
**Temporal Coverage:** Current - updated annually by operators[29]
**Update Frequency:** Annual operator submissions[28]
**Access Type:** Restricted - Public Map Viewer (county-level view) or PIMMA (requires authorization)[30][28]
**API Availability:** No - web viewer only, GIS data requires formal request[30]
**Official URL:** https://www.npms.phmsa.dot.gov[31]
**Quality Assessment:** Most comprehensive pipeline location data for the US. Includes gas transmission and hazardous liquid pipelines, LNG plants, and breakout tanks. Does NOT include gathering or distribution lines, interconnects, valves, or operational details. Public viewer limited to county-level access; full GIS data requires government/operator authorization. Essential but access-restricted dataset.[29][30][28]

### OpenStreetMap Pipelines
**Dataset Name:** OSM Pipeline Network  
**Source/Provider:** OpenStreetMap Community  
**Resolution/Scale:** Variable  
**Temporal Coverage:** Current  
**Update Frequency:** Real-time  
**Access Type:** Open/Free  
**API Availability:** Yes - Overpass API  
**Official URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org  
**Quality Assessment:** Generally incomplete for pipelines as most are not visible or publicly documented. May include above-ground sections and compressor stations. NOT suitable as primary pipeline data source.

## 8. PROTECTED AREAS / ENVIRONMENTAL

**Dataset Name:** PAD-US 3.0[32][33][34]
**Source/Provider:** U.S. Geological Survey Gap Analysis Project[33][32]
**Resolution/Scale:** Variable by jurisdiction - typically 1:24,000 or better  
**Temporal Coverage:** Current as of May 2023 (v3.0)[34]
**Update Frequency:** Major releases every 1-2 years[32]
**Access Type:** Open/Free[32]
**API Availability:** Limited - downloadable geodatabase, WMS services available[32]
**Official URL:** https://www.usgs.gov/programs/gap-analysis-project/science/pad-us-data-overview[32]

Includes 25+ attributes covering ownership, management, designation type, and GAP status codes. Five feature classes (Fee, Designation, Easement, Marine, Proclamation). Comprehensive coverage of National Parks, Wildlife Refuges, Wilderness Areas, Conservation Easements, and state/local protected areas. Essential for environmental constraint analysis.[35][33][32]

**Dataset Name:** NGMDB and State Geological Survey Maps[36][37]

**Access Type:** Open/Free[36]

**Official URL:** https://ngmdb.usgs.gov[37]
**Quality Assessment:** Comprehensive collection of geologic maps from USGS and state surveys. Variable coverage and detail across the country. Essential for understanding bedrock geology, structural features, and geologic hazards. Newer maps available digitally; many older maps being digitized.[36][37]

### OneGeology
**Dataset Name:** OneGeology Portal[40][41]
**Source/Provider:** International consortium including USGS[40]
**Resolution/Scale:** Variable by contributing survey  
**Temporal Coverage:** Current  
**Update Frequency:** Continuous  
**Access Type:** Open/Free[40]
**API Availability:** Yes - WMS/WFS services[42]
**Official URL:** https://portal.onegeology.org[41]
**Quality Assessment:** Provides access to global geological survey data through standardized web services. US coverage from USGS. Useful for cross-border projects and accessing state geological survey data through single portal.[41][40]

### NRCS Soil Survey Geographic Database (SSURGO)
**Dataset Name:** SSURGO and gSSURGO[43][44]
**Source/Provider:** USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS)[44][43]
**Resolution/Scale:** 1:12,000 to 1:63,360 (detailed soil mapping units)[45]
**Temporal Coverage:** Current - based on century of soil survey work[45]
**Update Frequency:** Continuous updates via Web Soil Survey, annual gSSURGO releases[44][45]
**Access Type:** Open/Free[43]
**API Availability:** Yes - Soil Data Access (SDA) API with REST/POST and WMS/WFS services[45]
**Official URL:** https://websoilsurvey.nrcs.usda.gov[46]
**Quality Assessment:** Most detailed soil data available for the US. Includes physical, chemical, and interpretive properties. gSSURGO provides statewide rasters (10m resolution by state, 30m for CONUS). 7,000-10,000 daily users. Extensive attribute tables with interpretations for construction, agriculture, and engineering. Essential for pipeline routing (corrosivity, depth to bedrock, drainage, bearing capacity). API access enables efficient large-area queries.[47][45]

### FAO Harmonized World Soil Database (HWSD)
**Dataset Name:** HWSD v1.2  
**Source/Provider:** FAO/IIASA  
**Resolution/Scale:** 1km (coarse)  
**Temporal Coverage:** Static  
**Update Frequency:** Infrequent updates  
**Access Type:** Open/Free  
**API Availability:** No  
**Official URL:** Available through FAO  
**Quality Assessment:** For US applications, SSURGO is vastly superior. HWSD useful only for international consistency or areas without SSURGO coverage.

## 10. CLIMATE / WEATHER

### NOAA National Weather Service API
**Dataset Name:** NWS API and Climate Data Online (CDO)[48][49]
**Source/Provider:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA)[49][48]
**Resolution/Scale:** Station-based observations plus gridded products  
**Temporal Coverage:** Historical (1800s to present) and real-time[50][51]
**Update Frequency:** Real-time for forecasts/observations; historical data continuously archived[48]
**Access Type:** Open/Free[49][48]
**API Availability:** Yes - NWS API (weather.gov/api), CDO API v2, REST services, THREDDS servers[52][48][49]
**Official URL:** https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api (NWS API)[48]
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/cdo-web/ (CDO)[51]
**Quality Assessment:** Comprehensive weather and climate data including temperature, precipitation, wind, degree days, extreme events, and 30-year normals. NWS API provides forecasts, alerts, and observations. CDO provides historical data from Global Historical Climatology Network (GHCNd). Multiple access methods (API, web services, bulk downloads). Excellent data quality with extensive station network. Essential for design parameters and construction planning.[50][51][49][48]

### ERA5 Reanalysis (Global)
**Dataset Name:** ERA5  
**Source/Provider:** ECMWF/Copernicus  
**Resolution/Scale:** ~31km (0.25 degree)  
**Temporal Coverage:** 1940-present  
**Update Frequency:** Near real-time (5-day lag)  
**Access Type:** Open/Free  
**API Availability:** Yes - Climate Data Store API  
**Official URL:** Available through Copernicus Climate Data Store  
**Quality Assessment:** Highest quality global reanalysis. Hourly data for hundreds of variables. Excellent for locations without ground stations and for obtaining consistent historical climatologies.

### WorldClim (Global)
**Dataset Name:** WorldClim v2.1  
**Source/Provider:** WorldClim.org  
**Resolution/Scale:** 30 arc-seconds (~1km) to 10 minutes  
**Temporal Coverage:** 1970-2000 averages, future projections  
**Update Frequency:** Periodic major releases  
**Access Type:** Open/Free  
**API Availability:** No - tile downloads  
**Official URL:** https://www.worldclim.org  
**Quality Assessment:** High-resolution interpolated climate surfaces. Good for spatial climate analysis. For US, PRISM climate data may provide higher quality.

### Airbus (Pléiades, SPOT)
**Dataset Name:** Airbus Defense and Space Imagery  
**Source/Provider:** Airbus

### Sentinel-2 (Free Baseline)
**Dataset Name:** Sentinel-2 A/B  
**Source/Provider:** European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme  
**Resolution/Scale:** 10m (visible/NIR), 20m (red edge/SWIR), 60m (coastal/cirrus)[62]

Cloud cover can be limiting factor. Essential baseline imagery for all projects.[63][62]

**Update Frequency:** As needed  
**Access Type:** Open/Free (unclassified boundaries)  
**API Availability:** Limited  
**Official URL:** Various DOD and FAA sources  
**Quality Assessment:** Identifies military bases, training ranges, and restricted airspace requiring coordination. Critical for security-sensitive routing.

### USDA CropScape / Cropland Data Layer
**Dataset Name:** Cropland Data Layer (CDL)  
**Source/Provider:** USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service  
**Resolution/Scale:** 30m  
**Temporal Coverage:** Annual from 1997-present  
**Update Frequency:** Annual  
**Access Type:** Open/Free  
**API Availability:** Yes - CropScape web service  
**Official URL:** https://nassgeodata.gmu.edu/CropScape  
**Quality Assessment:** Detailed agricultural land use with crop-specific classifications. Excellent accuracy (85-95%) for major crops. Useful for agricultural impact assessment and temporary workspace planning.

***

This comprehensive dataset inventory provides the foundation for high-quality oil and gas pipeline routing GIS analysis in the United States. Priority should be given to USGS 3DEP elevation data, NHDPlus HR for hydrology, PAD-US for environmental constraints, SSURGO for soils, Census TIGER for boundaries, and NPMS for existing pipeline locations (though access to full NPMS data requires authorization). The combination of these authoritative federal datasets with targeted commercial high-resolution imagery provides optimal data coverage for pipeline route analysis and permitting.
