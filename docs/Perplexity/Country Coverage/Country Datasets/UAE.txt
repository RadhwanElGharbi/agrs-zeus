# Comprehensive GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing in United Arab Emirates

## 1. Elevation / Terrain (DEM)

**Abu Dhabi Spatial Data Infrastructure (AD-SDI) DEM**
- **Source:** Abu Dhabi Systems and Information Centre
- **Resolution:** High resolution (specific resolution requires registration access)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular updates
- **Access:** Restricted (requires registration and approval)
- **API:** Yes (ArcGIS REST API through portal)
- **URL:** https://arcgis.sdi.abudhabi.ae/portal/home/
- **Quality:** High-quality authoritative emirate-level data for Abu Dhabi; likely the best available for that emirate with official government backing[1][2][3]

**GeoDubai DEM**
- **Source:** Dubai Municipality GIS Center
- **Resolution:** High resolution (specific details available upon registration)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current, updated regularly
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous updates to base map
- **Access:** Restricted (requires approval through GeoDubai portal)
- **API:** Yes (REST API available)
- **URL:** https://geodubai.dm.gov.ae/
- **Quality:** High-quality official data for Dubai emirate; integrated with Digital Twin initiative including 3D building models[4][5]

**Copernicus DEM (GLO-30)**
- **Source:** European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution:** 30m (GLO-30) and 90m (GLO-90) versions available
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2011-2020+
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic (stable product)
- **Access:** Open/Free (GLO-90 and GLO-30)
- **API:** Yes (OData API, S3 bucket access)
- **URL:** https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM
- **Quality:** Best global DEM for UAE; 30m resolution provides good accuracy for regional pipeline routing; minimal voids; globally consistent[6][7]

**Gulf Countries DEM (SRTM-based)**
- **Source:** NASA/USGS via Humanitarian Data Exchange
- **Resolution:** 250m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://data.humdata.org/dataset/gulf-countries-elevation-model
- **Quality:** Coarse resolution limits utility for detailed pipeline routing; useful for regional overview only[8]

**No national LiDAR-derived DEM datasets** were identified as publicly available, though Abu Dhabi and Dubai maintain high-resolution elevation data through their SDI programs that may include LiDAR.[1][4]

## 2. Land Cover / Land Use

**ESA WorldCover 2021**
- **Source:** European Space Agency
- **Resolution:** 10m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2021 (2020 also available)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes (available through Google Earth Engine and direct download)
- **URL:** https://worldcover2020.esa.int
- **Quality:** Excellent 10m resolution Sentinel-2 based product; 11 land cover classes; high accuracy for UAE's diverse landscapes including desert, urban, and coastal areas[9][10]

**Google Dynamic World (Sentinel-2)**
- **Source:** Google/WRI
- **Resolution:** 10m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present (near real-time)
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous (every 2-5 days with Sentinel-2)
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes (Google Earth Engine)
- **URL:** Available through Google Earth Engine
- **Quality:** Near real-time land cover; useful for monitoring rapid development in UAE; 9 land cover classes

**UAE National Land Use Data**
- **Source:** Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure (MOEI) / Emirates authorities
- **Resolution:** Variable by emirate
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular
- **Access:** Restricted (requires formal request through MOEI or emirate SDI portals)
- **API:** Limited (portal-based access)
- **URL:** https://opendata.moei.gov.ae (portal recently launched with 200+ datasets)
- **Quality:** Most authoritative for UAE; includes detailed urban planning and land classification data; access restricted[11][12]

## 3. Hydrology / Water

**UAE Hydrogeological Map**
- **Source:** Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure & Environment Agency Abu Dhabi
- **Resolution:** National scale
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2023 (latest release)
- **Update Frequency:** Major updates every few years
- **Access:** Restricted (requires formal request)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.moei.gov.ae/ (contact ministry)
- **Quality:** Authoritative national hydrogeological mapping including groundwater, wadis, and water resources; critical for pipeline routing[13]

**National Water Resources Database**
- **Source:** Ministry of Climate Change and Environment / UAE University research
- **Resolution:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical to current
- **Update Frequency:** Ongoing research updates
- **Access:** Restricted/Academic
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact MOCCAE or UAE University
- **Quality:** Research-grade data on water resources; limited public access[14][15]

**HydroSHEDS (Global)**
- **Source:** USGS / Conservation International
- **Resolution:** 15 arc-seconds (~450m at equator)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on SRTM 2000 data
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** No (direct download)
- **URL:** https://www.hydrosheds.org/
- **Quality:** Moderate quality for UAE wadis and drainage patterns; limited detail for ephemeral streams[16][17]

**Global Surface Water Explorer**
- **Source:** European Commission JRC
- **Resolution:** 30m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1984-present
- **Update Frequency:** Annual updates
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes (Google Earth Engine)
- **URL:** https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/
- **Quality:** Good for tracking permanent/seasonal water bodies; limited application for desert wadis

## 4. Infrastructure - Roads

**UAE National Road Network**
- **Source:** Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure
- **Resolution:** National standard
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous
- **Access:** Restricted (requires formal data request)
- **API:** Possible through MOEI Open Data Portal
- **URL:** https://opendata.moei.gov.ae
- **Quality:** Most authoritative and complete road network data; includes federal and local roads[12][18][11]

**HERE Maps (Commercial)**
- **Source:** HERE Technologies
- **Resolution:** Sub-meter accuracy in urban areas
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous (quarterly major releases)
- **Access:** Commercial/Purchase (licensing required)
- **API:** Yes (HERE REST APIs - Maps, Routing, Traffic)
- **URL:** https://www.here.com/
- **Quality:** Very high quality commercial dataset; excellent coverage of UAE road network including attributes, restrictions, and real-time traffic; used by UAE government agencies[19][20]

**TomTom MultiNet (Commercial)**
- **Source:** TomTom
- **Resolution:** Sub-meter in urban areas
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Quarterly updates
- **Access:** Commercial/Purchase
- **API:** Yes (TomTom Maps API, Traffic API)
- **URL:** https://www.tomtom.com/
- **Quality:** Comprehensive coverage across UAE (84 countries total network); real-time traffic data from 600+ million devices; widely used in UAE for traffic management and ITS systems[21][20][22][23]

**OpenStreetMap UAE Roads**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap Community
- **Resolution:** Variable (sub-meter where traced)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (continuously updated)
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time (crowdsourced)
- **Access:** Open/Free (ODbL license)
- **API:** Yes (Overpass API, OSM API)
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/ or https://data.humdata.org/dataset/hotosm_are_roads
- **Quality:** Approximately 74% complete compared to AI estimates (~126,900 km mapped); good coverage of major roads and urban areas; excellent for Abu Dhabi and Dubai; some rural areas incomplete; free alternative but requires validation[24][25][26]

## 6. Infrastructure - Utilities (Power, Telecom)

**UAE Electricity Transmission Network**
- **Source:** Federal Electricity and Water Authority (FEWA) / Emirates utilities
- **Resolution:** Engineering grade
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** As infrastructure changes
- **Access:** Restricted (requires formal utility coordination)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact FEWA or Abu Dhabi/Dubai utilities
- **Quality:** Critical infrastructure data; highly restricted access; essential for pipeline routing to avoid conflicts; must be obtained through official channels[27]

**OpenInfraMap / OpenStreetMap Utilities**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap Community

- **Temporal Coverage:** Current

## 7. Infrastructure - Pipelines

**ADNOC Pipeline GIS Data**
- **Source:** Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC Gas Processing)
- **Resolution:** Sub-meter survey accuracy (±15cm tolerance in recent surveys)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous updates from surveys and construction
- **Access:** Highly Restricted (internal ADNOC use; external access requires formal agreements)
- **API:** Internal GIS systems only
- **URL:** Contact ADNOC directly for data sharing agreements
- **Quality:** Highest quality pipeline data for Abu Dhabi region; over 3,260 km network; surveyed using GPS, GPR, and traditional methods; includes above-ground and underground pipelines with corridor features; 95% sub-meter horizontal accuracy, 97% sub-meter depth accuracy; authoritative but difficult to access[28][29][30]

**UAE Government Pipeline Registry**
- **Source:** Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure
- **Resolution:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Regulatory reporting basis
- **Access:** Restricted (requires formal request and justification)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.moei.gov.ae/
- **Quality:** Regulatory data on pipeline infrastructure; access highly controlled for security reasons[12]

**OpenStreetMap UAE Pipelines**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap Community
- **Resolution:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Community-driven
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes (Overpass API)
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/
- **Quality:** Very incomplete; not suitable for pipeline routing planning; only visible above-ground infrastructure likely mapped; security concerns limit mapping[25]

## 8. Protected Areas / Environmental

**UAE Protected Areas (National)**
- **Source:** Ministry of Climate Change and Environment (MOCCAE) / Environment Agency Abu Dhabi
- **Resolution:** High resolution (polygon boundaries)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** As designations change
- **Access:** Restricted/Open (some data via MOCCAE Open Data)
- **API:** Limited
- **URL:** https://www.moccae.gov.ae/en/open-data/moccae-open-data or https://bayanat.ae/
- **Quality:** Most authoritative for UAE protected areas; includes national parks, reserves, wildlife sanctuaries, and marine protected areas[14]

**World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA)**
- **Source:** UNEP-WCMC and IUCN
- **Resolution:** Polygon boundaries
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (updated monthly)
- **Update Frequency:** Monthly
- **Access:** Open/Free (with some restrictions on commercial use)
- **API:** Yes (available through Google Earth Engine and IBAT)
- **URL:** https://www.protectedplanet.net/en/country/ARE
- **Quality:** Global standard dataset; good coverage of UAE's protected areas; over 300,000 sites globally; regularly updated; suitable for constraint analysis[31][32][33][34]

## 9. Geology / Soil

**British Geological Survey UAE Geological Maps**
- **Source:** British Geological Survey (commissioned by UAE Ministry of Energy)
- **Resolution:** 1:250,000 scale
- **Temporal Coverage:** Completed 1970s-1990s (historical)
- **Update Frequency:** Static (historical product)
- **Access:** Restricted/Purchase (contact BGS)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://webapps.bgs.ac.uk/data/Publications/series.html?code=RS
- **Quality:** Comprehensive geological mapping of UAE at regional scale; authoritative bedrock geology; dated but still foundational reference[35][36]

**USGS Arabian Peninsula Geology**
- **Source:** U.S. Geological Survey
- **Resolution:** Regional scale (1:2,000,000+)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://certmapper.cr.usgs.gov/data/apps/world-maps/ or https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/bedrock-geology-of-the-arabian-peninsula-and-selected-adjacent-areas-geo2bg
- **Quality:** Regional overview geology; too coarse for detailed pipeline routing but useful for understanding regional geological provinces[37][38][39]

**FAO Harmonized World Soil Database (HWSD)**
- **Source:** Food and Agriculture Organization
- **Resolution:** 1 km
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on various sources (1960s-2000s)
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** No
- **URL:** http://www.fao.org/soils-portal/
- **Quality:** Moderate resolution global soil database; limited detail for UAE's varied desert soils; useful for general soil type identification

**OneGeology**
- **Source:** International geological surveys collaboration
- **Resolution:** Variable by country (UAE limited contribution)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Variable
- **Update Frequency:** Depends on national survey contributions
- **Access:** Open/Free (web services)
- **API:** Yes (WMS/WFS services)
- **URL:** http://www.onegeology.org/
- **Quality:** Limited UAE-specific data; primarily links to existing geological survey products

## 10. Climate / Weather

**UAE National Center of Meteorology (NCM)**
- **Source:** National Center of Meteorology UAE
- **Resolution:** Station-based and gridded products
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical archive and current/forecast
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time (10-minute updates), daily archives
- **Access:** Restricted (requires API registration and approval)
- **API:** Yes (NCM API v2.0 with authentication)
- **URL:** https://api.ncm.gov.ae/documentation/
- **Quality:** Highest quality national meteorological data; current weather, forecasts, historical climate; authoritative for UAE; API requires registration[40][41]

**ERA5 Reanalysis**
- **Source:** European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) via Copernicus
- **Resolution:** 0.25° (~25-30 km)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1940-present
- **Update Frequency:** Monthly updates (2-3 month lag)
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes (CDS API)
- **URL:** https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/
- **Quality:** Excellent global reanalysis product; comprehensive climate variables; hourly data; good for UAE climate baseline and historical analysis

**WorldClim v2**
- **Source:** WorldClim project (University of California)
- **Resolution:** 30 arc-seconds (~1 km at equator)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical averages (1970-2000) and future scenarios
- **Update Frequency:** Static climatologies
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** No (direct download)
- **URL:** https://www.worldclim.org/
- **Quality:** Good for long-term climate normals; temperature and precipitation averages; useful for pipeline design climate parameters

## 12. Population / Demographics

**UAE Census Data (FCSC)**
- **Source:** Federal Competitiveness and Statistics Centre
- **Resolution:** Variable (district to grid level)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2005, 2015, 2021+ censuses
- **Update Frequency:** Census every 10 years, estimates annually
- **Access:** Restricted (some data via 1Map platform)
- **API:** Limited (through 1Map ArcGIS services)
- **URL:** https://www.fcsc.gov.ae/ or https://atlas.fgic.gov.ae/
- **Quality:** Official census data; most accurate population counts and demographics; limited spatial granularity in published data[43][42]

**Dubai Statistics Center**
- **Source:** Dubai Statistics Center
- **Resolution:** District and community level
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Annual updates
- **Access:** Open (some datasets)
- **API:** Limited
- **URL:** https://www.dsc.gov.ae/
- **Quality:** Detailed demographic data for Dubai emirate; good spatial linkage to administrative boundaries[46]

**WorldPop UAE**
- **Source:** WorldPop / University of Southampton
- **Resolution:** 100m (constrained) and 1 km (unconstrained)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000-2020 (annual)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual releases
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes (WorldPop API)
- **URL:** https://hub.worldpop.org/
- **Quality:** High-resolution gridded population estimates; 100m version is building-footprint constrained; Random Forests methodology; UN-adjusted totals; excellent for spatial population distribution analysis; preferred global dataset for UAE[47][48][49][50]

**LandScan Global**
- **Source:** Oak Ridge National Laboratory
- **Resolution:** 1 km (approximately 30 arc-seconds)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Annual (2000-present)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access:** Free (registration required) for select countries; commercial for others
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://landscan.ornl.gov/
- **Quality:** Ambient population model (daytime distribution); good for infrastructure planning; moderate resolution

## 13. Satellite Imagery (Commercial & Free)

**Maxar High-Resolution Imagery**
- **Source:** Maxar Technologies (WorldView constellation, Legion satellites)
- **Resolution:** 30-50 cm (commercial), up to 15 cm (government programs)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Archive from 2000s + tasking
- **Update Frequency:** Tasking available (up to 15 revisits/day for some locations)
- **Access:** Commercial/Purchase (licensing required)
- **API:** Yes (through partners like UP42, or direct Maxar API)
- **URL:** https://www.maxar.com/ or https://up42.com/marketplace/data/tasking/maxar-tasking
- **Quality:** Industry-leading very high-resolution imagery; excellent for UAE; stereo capability; near real-time tasking; most widely used commercial imagery[51][52]

**Airbus Defense and Space (Pléiades, SPOT)**
- **Source:** Airbus Intelligence
- **Resolution:** 50 cm (Pléiades Neo, Pléiades), 1.5m (SPOT 6/7)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Archive + tasking
- **Update Frequency:** Tasking available (daily revisit for Pléiades constellation)
- **Access:** Commercial/Purchase
- **API:** Yes (OneAtlas API, available through UP42)
- **URL:** https://www.airbus.com/en/products-services/space/earth-observation/satellite-imagery or via UP42
- **Quality:** High-quality European commercial imagery; good UAE coverage; stereo capability; competitive pricing[53][54]

**Planet Labs**
- **Source:** Planet Labs
- **Resolution:** 3-5m (PlanetScope), 50 cm (SkySat)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Daily global coverage (PlanetScope), archive + tasking (SkySat)
- **Update Frequency:** Daily (PlanetScope), tasking available (SkySat)
- **Access:** Commercial/Purchase (subscription-based)
- **API:** Yes (Planet API)
- **URL:** https://www.planet.com/
- **Quality:** Unique high-frequency coverage; daily monitoring capability; good for change detection and monitoring; moderate resolution for detailed planning[55]

**Sentinel-2 (Free)**
- **Source:** European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution:** 10m (RGB, NIR), 20m (red edge, SWIR), 60m (atmospheric)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present
- **Update Frequency:** 5-day revisit (2 satellites)
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes (Copernicus Data Space API, Google Earth Engine, AWS)
- **URL:** https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/ or https://scihub.copernicus.eu/
- **Quality:** Best free multispectral imagery; 10m resolution suitable for regional pipeline corridor analysis; excellent for change detection and land cover mapping; reliable and consistent

## 14. Special Datasets for UAE Pipeline Routing

**Archaeological and Heritage Sites**
- **Source:** Ministry of Culture and Youth / Department of Culture and Tourism Abu Dhabi
- **Resolution:** Point and polygon locations
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** As sites are registered
- **Access:** Restricted (cultural heritage protection)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact ministry or DCT Abu Dhabi
- **Quality:** Critical for pipeline routing to avoid cultural heritage sites; access restricted to protect sensitive locations; formal consultation required for pipeline projects

**Military Zones and Restricted Areas**
- **Source:** UAE Armed Forces / Ministry of Defence
- **Resolution:** Polygon boundaries
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** As restrictions change
- **Access:** Highly Restricted (security classification)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Formal coordination with military authorities required
- **Quality:** Essential data for pipeline routing; must be obtained through official security clearance channels; significant portions of UAE have military restrictions

**Seismic Hazard Data**
- **Source:** National Seismic Network (operated by USGS for UAE)
- **Resolution:** Station network data and regional hazard maps
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical seismicity records
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous monitoring, hazard maps updated periodically
- **Access:** Limited (contact NCM or relevant authorities)
- **API:** Limited
- **URL:** Contact National Center of Meteorology or check USGS International Programs
- **Quality:** UAE is located in relatively low seismic zone but data important for pipeline design standards; GSN station exists in UAE[56]

**Sabkha (Salt Flat) Mapping**
- **Source:** Various research institutions and geological surveys
- **Resolution:** Variable (regional to detailed studies)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Various research periods
- **Update Frequency:** Research-dependent
- **Access:** Academic/Restricted
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact UAE University, Geological Survey, or Environment Agency Abu Dhabi
- **Quality:** Critical for pipeline routing; sabkhas present significant engineering challenges (corrosive, unstable ground); localized datasets available through research channels[57][58]

**Sand Dune and Aeolian Features**
- **Source:** Remote sensing analysis and field surveys
- **Resolution:** Variable (derived from satellite imagery and DEM)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Dynamic features requiring periodic monitoring
- **Access:** Can be derived from satellite imagery
- **API:** No specific API (derive from imagery)
- **URL:** Derive from Sentinel-2, Planet, or commercial imagery
- **Quality:** Important for pipeline routing in desert areas; dune migration can impact infrastructure; can be mapped from high-resolution DEMs and multispectral imagery[58]

**UAE Groundwater Vulnerability Maps**
- **Source:** Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure / Environment Agency Abu Dhabi
- **Resolution:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on hydrogeological map (2023)
- **Update Frequency:** Major updates every few years
- **Access:** Restricted (formal request required)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact MOEI or EAD
- **Quality:** Important for environmental impact assessment; groundwater protection zones critical for pipeline routing decisions[13]

**Flash Flood Hazard / Wadi Flow Paths**
- **Source:** Derived from DEM analysis / NCM studies
- **Resolution:** Variable (DEM-dependent, typically 30m from Copernicus)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on historical rainfall and DEM
- **Update Frequency:** Can be updated as better DEMs become available
- **Access:** Can be derived or requested from NCM/MOEI
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Derive from terrain analysis or contact meteorology/water authorities
- **Quality:** Critical for pipeline crossing design; UAE experiences occasional intense rainfall and flash flooding in wadis; can be modeled from high-quality DEMs[40][13]

**UAE Road and Aviation Development Plans**
- **Source:** Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure / Civil Aviation Authority
- **Resolution:** Project-specific
- **Temporal Coverage:** Future planning horizons
- **Update Frequency:** As plans are updated
- **Access:** Restricted (project coordination required)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.moei.gov.ae/ or infrastructure development authorities
- **Quality:** Essential for future-proofing pipeline routes; coordination with planned infrastructure development required[18][12]

### Key Access Recommendations

**For pipeline routing projects in the UAE, prioritize:**

1. **Official national/emirate data** through formal requests to MOEI, Abu Dhabi SDI, and GeoDubai for authoritative datasets
2. **ADNOC coordination** for existing pipeline infrastructure (mandatory for Abu Dhabi operations)
3. **Commercial road data** (HERE or TomTom) for comprehensive infrastructure mapping with regular updates
4. **Copernicus DEM (30m)** and **ESA WorldCover (10m)** as best free global datasets for terrain and land cover
5. **WorldPop (100m)** for high-resolution population distribution
6. **WDPA** for protected areas as starting point, verify with national environmental agencies
7. **Maxar or Airbus imagery** for detailed corridor surveys and validation
8. **Sentinel-2** for free baseline imagery and change detection monitoring

Most critical national datasets require formal data sharing agreements and security clearances due to the sensitive nature of infrastructure and security concerns in the UAE.[28][12][1]
