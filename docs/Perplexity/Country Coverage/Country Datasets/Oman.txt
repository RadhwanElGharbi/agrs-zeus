# GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing in Oman

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### National Sources

**National Survey and Geospatial Information Authority (NSGIA) Topographic DEMs**
- **Source/Provider**: National Survey and Geospatial Information Authority (formerly NSA)
- **Resolution/Scale**: 1:50,000 and 1:100,000 scale topographic maps with derived DEMs
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current (established geodetic infrastructure ONGD17 in 2017)
- **Update Frequency**: As needed for strategic projects
- **Access Type**: Restricted/requires formal written request to NSGIA
- **API Availability**: No public API
- **Official URL**: https://www.nsaomangeoportal.gov.om/en/
- **Quality Assessment**: NSGIA is the sole authority for geospatial data in Oman with complete national coverage; accuracy based on OmanCORSNet (47 CORS stations) achieving cm-level accuracy; includes OmanGeoid vertical datum for accurate orthometric heights[1][2][3]

### Global DEM Sources

**Copernicus GLO-30 DEM (Highest Priority Global)**
- **Source/Provider**: European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution/Scale**: 30m (1 arc-second)
- **Temporal Coverage**: 2010-2015 (TanDEM-X mission data)
- **Update Frequency**: Static baseline product
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes - via Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem API
- **Official URL**: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM
- **Quality Assessment**: Best available global DEM; Digital Surface Model (DSM) from radar data; superior to SRTM and ASTER GDEM for most applications; RMSE typically 2-6m vertical accuracy; void-filled using multiple sources including ASTER GDEM and SRTM[4][5][6][7]

**SRTM v3.0 (30m)**
- **Source/Provider**: NASA/USGS
- **Resolution/Scale**: 30m (1 arc-second) for non-US areas
- **Temporal Coverage**: February 2000 (single mission)
- **Update Frequency**: Static (no updates)
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes - via USGS Earth Explorer API and Google Earth Engine
- **Official URL**: https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/
- **Quality Assessment**: Good baseline accuracy (~6-9m RMSE); radar-based; voids filled in v3.0; reliable for Oman's terrain but lower quality than Copernicus DEM[8][4]

**ASTER GDEM v3**
- **Source/Provider**: NASA/METI (Japan)
- **Resolution/Scale**: 30m (1 arc-second)
- **Temporal Coverage**: 2000-2013 (compiled from multiple acquisitions)
- **Update Frequency**: Static (v3 released 2019)
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes - via NASA Earthdata API
- **Official URL**: https://asterweb.jpl.nasa.gov/gdem.asp
- **Quality Assessment**: Lower quality than radar-based DEMs due to optical imaging artifacts; useful as supplementary source in mountainous areas; contains noise and anomalies; not recommended as primary source for pipeline routing[5][4]

## 2. LAND COVER / LAND USE

### National Sources

**Ministry of Agriculture and Fisheries Land Use Maps**
- **Source/Provider**: Ministry of Agriculture and Fisheries (MAF) / Supreme Committee for Town Planning
- **Resolution/Scale**: 1:100,000 scale general coverage
- **Temporal Coverage**: Completed 1990 (UNDP/FAO project)
- **Update Frequency**: Unknown, likely outdated
- **Access Type**: Restricted/requires formal request
- **API Availability**: No
- **Official URL**: Not publicly available
- **Quality Assessment**: Dated baseline; complete national coverage but needs verification with recent imagery; compiled with UN support but now over 30 years old[3]

### Global Sources (Recommended)

**ESA WorldCover 2021 (Best Global Option)**
- **Source/Provider**: European Space Agency (ESA)
- **Resolution/Scale**: 10m resolution
- **Temporal Coverage**: 2020 and 2021 annual products
- **Update Frequency**: Annual (2020, 2021 released; future updates planned)
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: No dedicated API, but downloadable via web interface
- **Official URL**: https://worldcover2021.esa.int/download
- **Quality Assessment**: Highest resolution global land cover (10m); 76.7% overall accuracy for 2021 product; 11 land cover classes; based on Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 data; excellent for Oman's arid/semi-arid environments; freely accessible with area statistics tools[9][10][11][12]

**Google Dynamic World**
- **Source/Provider**: Google/World Resources Institute
- **Resolution/Scale**: 10m resolution
- **Temporal Coverage**: 2015-present (near real-time)
- **Update Frequency**: Continuous (updated as Sentinel-2 imagery becomes available)
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes - via Google Earth Engine
- **Official URL**: https://www.dynamicworld.app/
- **Quality Assessment**: Near real-time land cover with probability scores; 10m resolution matching Sentinel-2; 9 land cover classes; useful for monitoring land use changes during pipeline planning

**Sentinel-2 Land Cover Classification**
- **Source/Provider**: ESA Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution/Scale**: 10-20m resolution (depending on band)
- **Temporal Coverage**: 2015-present
- **Update Frequency**: 5-day revisit time (2 satellite constellation)
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes - Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem API, Google Earth Engine
- **Official URL**: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/
- **Quality Assessment**: Raw multispectral imagery (13 bands) allowing custom classification; 10m resolution for visible/NIR bands; excellent for detailed land cover mapping in Oman; requires processing but provides most current data[13]

## 3. HYDROLOGY / WATER

### National Sources

**Ministry of Water Resources - Hydrological and Hydrogeological Database**
- **Source/Provider**: Ministry of Agricultural, Fisheries and Water Resources (formerly Ministry of Water Resources)
- **Resolution/Scale**: Variable by dataset
- **Temporal Coverage**: Historical to current
- **Update Frequency**: Ongoing monitoring at gauging stations
- **Access Type**: Restricted/requires formal request
- **API Availability**: No public API
- **Official URL**: https://gov.om/en/w/request-hydrogeological-and-hydrological-data
- **Quality Assessment**: Includes wadi gauging stations, groundwater monitoring networks, and surface water data; established monitoring stations on northern side of Jebel mountains; meteorological stations at Saik, Nizwa, Rumais, and Rostak; data request service available but requires formal application[14][15][16]

**Oman National Hydrographic Office (ONHO)**
- **Source/Provider**: Royal Navy of Oman - Directorate of Oman National Hydrographic Office
- **Resolution/Scale**: Multiple scales for coastal/marine areas
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current, includes historical archives
- **Update Frequency**: Regular updates via monthly notices to mariners
- **Access Type**: Restricted/requires request for non-navigational data
- **API Availability**: No
- **Official URL**: Contact through Royal Navy of Oman
- **Quality Assessment**: Maintains National Hydrographic Archive with various surveys dating back decades; primarily focused on marine/coastal waters; includes tidal data and coastal surveys[17][18]

### Global Sources

**Global Surface Water Explorer (Google/JRC)**
- **Source/Provider**: European Commission Joint Research Centre / Google
- **Resolution/Scale**: 30m resolution
- **Temporal Coverage**: 1984-present
- **Update Frequency**: Annual updates
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes - Google Earth Engine
- **Official URL**: https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/
- **Quality Assessment**: Excellent for identifying permanent and seasonal water bodies, wadis with intermittent flow; particularly valuable for Oman's arid environment; Landsat-based time series shows water occurrence, seasonality, and change

**HydroSHEDS / HydroRIVERS**
- **Source/Provider**: World Wildlife Fund / McGill University
- **Resolution/Scale**: Multiple resolutions (3", 15", 30")
- **Temporal Coverage**: Based on SRTM 2000 data
- **Update Frequency**: Static baseline
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Direct download only
- **Official URL**: https://www.hydrosheds.org/
- **Quality Assessment**: Hydrologically conditioned DEM derivatives; includes stream networks, watersheds, flow directions; moderate quality for Oman's wadis but may miss ephemeral channels; useful for watershed delineation

### OpenStreetMap

**OpenStreetMap Oman Roads**

**Microsoft AI Roads**

- **Resolution/Scale**: Extracted from high-resolution satellite imagery
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current snapshot
- **Update Frequency**: Periodic updates
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Direct download
- **Official URL**: https://data.nextgis.com/en/region/OM/msrd/
- **Quality Assessment**: AI-extracted road centerlines from satellite imagery; useful for identifying unmapped roads; requires validation but provides complementary coverage to OSM[23]

## 10. CLIMATE / WEATHER

### National Sources

**Directorate General of Meteorology (Oman Meteorological Service)**
- **Source/Provider**: Directorate General of Meteorology, Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology
- **Resolution/Scale**: Point data from weather stations
- **Temporal Coverage**: Historical records dating to ~1900 (systematic observations); modern network from 1970s
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time observations, daily records, monthly climatology
- **Access Type**: Some data via open data portal (https://data.gov.om/bixytwb/weather); detailed data may require formal request
- **API Availability**: Limited/unknown for detailed data
- **Official URL**: https://data.gov.om/ and contact DG Meteorology
- **Quality Assessment**: National meteorological authority with ground-based station network; includes temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind data; stations at key locations including Muscat, Salalah, Sohar, interior locations; climate indicators available through government data portal; most authoritative for Oman-specific weather patterns including cyclone/monsoon impacts; real-time and forecast data available[16][46]

### Global Sources (Recommended)

**ERA5 Reanalysis (Best Global Option)**
- **Source/Provider**: European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) / Copernicus Climate Change Service
- **Resolution/Scale**: 31km (0.25° x 0.25°) spatial resolution
- **Temporal Coverage**: January 1940 to present (3-month lag)
- **Update Frequency**: Monthly updates (extends continuously)
- **Access Type**: Open/Free (registration required)
- **API Availability**: Yes - Climate Data Store (CDS) API
- **Official URL**: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels
- **Quality Assessment**: Fifth-generation ECMWF reanalysis; hourly estimates for large number of climate variables; 137 vertical levels; combines model with global observations; excellent for Oman climate analysis (1950-2023 record available via World Bank Climate Portal); suitable for temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity analysis for pipeline design; daily and monthly aggregates available; superior to previous reanalysis products; accessible through multiple platforms including Google Earth Engine[47][48][49][50][51][52][53]

**WorldClim v2.1**
- **Source/Provider**: WorldClim / University of California
- **Resolution/Scale**: Multiple resolutions (30 seconds ~1km to 2.5 minutes)
- **Temporal Coverage**: Historical climate averages (1970-2000) and future projections
- **Update Frequency**: Periodic updates with new climatology periods
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Direct download only
- **Official URL**: https://www.worldclim.org/
- **Quality Assessment**: High-resolution interpolated climate surfaces; includes temperature (min/max/mean), precipitation, solar radiation, wind, water vapor; monthly averages useful for long-term climatology; widely used in climate modeling; good baseline for Oman but ERA5 provides more detailed temporal coverage

**Sentinel-2 (Recommended Free Option)**
- **Source/Provider**: European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution/Scale**: 10m (visible/NIR), 20m (red edge/SWIR), 60m (atmospheric bands)
- **Temporal Coverage**: 2015-present
- **Update Frequency**: 5-day revisit (2-satellite constellation); cloud-free composites vary
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes - Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem, Google Earth Engine, AWS
- **Official URL**: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/
- **Quality Assessment**: Excellent free baseline imagery; 13 spectral bands; 10m resolution suitable for detailed mapping; 5-day revisit ideal for monitoring; multispectral capabilities support land cover classification, vegetation monitoring; Level-2A atmospherically corrected products available; complete coverage of Oman; industry standard for free satellite data[13]

### High-Resolution Commercial Imagery

**Maxar Technologies (WorldView/GeoEye Constellation)**
- **Source/Provider**: Maxar Technologies
- **Resolution/Scale**: 30-50cm resolution (WorldView-3/4); sub-50cm multispectral
- **Temporal Coverage**: Extensive archive from 2000s; current imagery
- **Update Frequency**: On-demand tasking; archive coverage varies
- **Access Type**: Commercial/Purchase (per km² or subscription)
- **API Availability**: Yes - Maxar Platform API, SecureWatch API
- **Official URL**: https://www.maxar.com/
- **Quality Assessment**: Highest resolution commercial optical imagery; excellent for detailed pipeline route surveys, identifying obstacles, infrastructure conflicts; 30cm panchromatic, 1.2m multispectral with pansharpening to 30cm; WorldView-3 includes 8-band multispectral and 8-band SWIR; extensive archive for Oman; premium pricing; industry standard for engineering applications[60]

**Airbus Defence and Space (Pléiades, SPOT)**
- **Source/Provider**: Airbus Defence and Space
- **Resolution/Scale**: 50cm (Pléiades Neo); 70cm (Pléiades 1A/1B); 1.5m (SPOT 6/7)
- **Temporal Coverage**: Archive and current tasking
- **Update Frequency**: On-demand tasking
- **Access Type**: Commercial/Purchase
- **API Availability**: Yes - OneAtlas API
- **Official URL**: https://www.airbus.com/en/products-services/space/earth-observation
- **Quality Assessment**: High-quality commercial imagery with 50cm resolution (Pléiades Neo); 70+ Earth observation satellites operated; excellent coverage and rapid tasking capability; competitive with Maxar; strong presence in Middle East; Pléiades constellation provides tri-stereo capability for 3D modeling[61][62]

**Planet Labs**
- **Source/Provider**: Planet Labs
- **Resolution/Scale**: 3-5m (PlanetScope daily constellation); 50cm (SkySat)
- **Temporal Coverage**: Daily global coverage (PlanetScope); SkySat archive and tasking
- **Update Frequency**: Daily (PlanetScope); frequent revisit (SkySat)
- **Access Type**: Commercial/Subscription or purchase
- **API Availability**: Yes - Planet API
- **Official URL**: https://www.planet.com/
- **Quality Assessment**: Unique daily imaging capability with PlanetScope constellation (180+ satellites); 3-5m resolution suitable for monitoring and change detection; SkySat provides 50cm resolution for detailed surveys; excellent for temporal analysis and monitoring during construction; more affordable than Maxar/Airbus for monitoring applications

**21st Century Aerospace Technology (21AT) - TripleSat/BJ-3**
- **Source/Provider**: 21AT (Singapore-based)
- **Resolution/Scale**: 30cm (BJ-3A/B); 80cm (TripleSat)
- **Temporal Coverage**: 2021-present (BJ-3); 2015-present (TripleSat)
- **Update Frequency**: Regular revisit; tasking available
- **Access Type**: Commercial/Purchase
- **API Availability**: Contact provider
- **Official URL**: Contact through regional distributors
- **Quality Assessment**: Competitive high-resolution option; 30cm BJ-3 constellation launched 2021-2022; TripleSat constellation follows same orbit with 33-minute spacing; growing presence in Middle East market; potentially more competitive pricing than US/European providers[60]

## 14. SPECIAL DATASETS FOR OMAN

### Seismic Hazard

**Sultan Qaboos University - Earthquake Monitoring Center**
- **Source/Provider**: Sultan Qaboos University Earthquake Monitoring Center
- **Resolution/Scale**: Seismic source zones and hazard maps at regional scale
- **Temporal Coverage**: Historical earthquakes from 734 CE; instrumental record ~15 years; hazard maps for return periods 475/2475 years
- **Update Frequency**: Research updates; monitoring network continuous
- **Access Type**: Published research accessible; detailed data requires academic collaboration
- **API Availability**: No
- **Official URL**: Contact Sultan Qaboos University
- **Quality Assessment**: Comprehensive seismic hazard assessment for Oman; 26 seismic source zones identified (Oman Mountains, Oman Sea, Zagros, Makran); PSHA studies completed for major economic zones (Duqm); PGA maps available; critical for pipeline design in seismically active areas; site-specific studies conducted for major developments; incorporates historical and instrumental seismicity; Makran and Zagros zones significant contributors to hazard[63][64][65][66]

**Site-Specific Seismic Studies**
- **Source/Provider**: Various geotechnical consultants / research institutions
- **Resolution/Scale**: Site-specific for major developments
- **Temporal Coverage**: Project-specific
- **Update Frequency**: Per project
- **Access Type**: Restricted/project-specific
- **API Availability**: No
- **Quality Assessment**: Detailed PSHA studies available for economic zones (e.g., Duqm SEZAD); includes VS30 mapping, amplification factors, soil liquefaction potential; comprehensive for major industrial areas; requires commissioning for specific pipeline routes

### Archaeological and Heritage Sites

**Ministry of Heritage and Tourism**
- **Source/Provider**: Ministry of Heritage and Tourism
- **Resolution/Scale**: Point and polygon data for registered sites
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current inventory
- **Update Frequency**: As sites are documented/registered
- **Access Type**: Restricted; requires formal consultation for development projects
- **API Availability**: No
- **Official URL**: Contact Ministry of Heritage and Tourism
- **Quality Assessment**: Official registry of archaeological sites, UNESCO World Heritage sites (including ancient cities, forts, aflaj irrigation systems); mandatory consultation for infrastructure projects; Oman has significant archaeological heritage including ancient copper mining sites, frankincense trade routes, Islamic architecture; critical for pipeline routing to avoid protected sites; requires early consultation in planning process

### Military Zones and Restricted Areas

**Ministry of Defence / Royal Oman Police**
- **Source/Provider**: Ministry of Defence, Royal Oman Police
- **Resolution/Scale**: Precise boundaries (classified/restricted)
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current operational areas
- **Update Frequency**: As security requirements change
- **Access Type**: Highly restricted; requires government-level coordination
- **API Availability**: No
- **Quality Assessment**: Military installations, training areas, security zones not publicly mapped; requires formal coordination through government channels; critical clearance needed early in pipeline planning; coastal areas may have naval restrictions; border areas with sensitive security considerations

### Ophiolite and Mineral Resources

**Ministry of Energy and Minerals - Mineral Concessions**
- **Source/Provider**: Ministry of Energy and Minerals
- **Resolution/Scale**: Detailed concession boundaries and mineral occurrence maps
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current mining concessions and exploration licenses
- **Update Frequency**: As concessions are granted/updated
- **Access Type**: Concession map publicly viewable; detailed data restricted
- **API Availability**: No
- **Official URL**: https://mem.gov.om/public/minerals
- **Quality Assessment**: Oman's unique ophiolite geology (Semail Ophiolite) includes economically significant chromite, copper, nickel laterites; mineral concessions and exploration areas must be avoided or coordinated; geological maps show ophiolite distribution, mineral deposits; important for identifying geotechnical challenges and avoiding resource conflicts[42][29]

### Wadi Flood Hazard

**Wadi Monitoring Network**
- **Source/Provider**: Ministry of Water Resources / Ministry of Environment
- **Resolution/Scale**: Gauging station network
- **Temporal Coverage**: Historical flood records; real-time monitoring at key wadis
- **Update Frequency**: Real-time during events; historical analysis periodic
- **Access Type**: Restricted; requires formal request
- **API Availability**: No
- **Quality Assessment**: Critical for Oman's arid environment with flashflood risk; gauging stations on northern Jebel wadis; ephemeral stream networks require special consideration; historical records of wadi flows essential for pipeline crossing design; climate change increasing cyclone intensity affecting Oman coastal areas[14]

### Land Ownership and Cadastre

**Ministry of Housing - Cadastral Survey**
- **Source/Provider**: Ministry of Housing
- **Resolution/Scale**: Parcel-level precision
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current registered properties
- **Update Frequency**: Continuous as properties are registered
- **Access Type**: Restricted; requires formal request for development projects
- **API Availability**: No
- **Official URL**: Contact Ministry of Housing
- **Quality Assessment**: Official land ownership records; essential for right-of-way acquisition; integrated with NSGIA survey framework; formal cadastral surveying conducted by Ministry of Housing; early consultation required for pipeline route corridor[3]

### Oman National Spatial Data Infrastructure (ONSDI)

**National SDI Framework**
- **Source/Provider**: NSGIA / Government of Oman
- **Resolution/Scale**: Framework for all geospatial data
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current infrastructure
- **Update Frequency**: Ongoing development
- **Access Type**: Controlled through Oman Geospatial Manual policies
- **API Availability**: Limited
- **Official URL**: https://www.nsaomangeoportal.gov.om/en/
- **Quality Assessment**: Comprehensive framework including ONGD17 datum, OmanCORSNet (47 CORS stations), OmanGeoid vertical datum, standardized procedures; Oman Geospatial Manual provides specifications for all geospatial activities; formal data request process through NSGIA; integration of datasets from various ministries; supports e-services and data sharing between government entities; most reliable path to accessing official government geospatial data[54][1][2]

## Key Recommendations for Oman Pipeline Routing:

1. **Start with NSGIA**: Formal data request to National Survey and Geospatial Information Authority is essential for accessing authoritative national datasets (topography, roads, administrative boundaries)[1][2]

2. **Use Copernicus DEM**: Best free global elevation data (30m) with superior quality to SRTM and ASTER GDEM[6][4]

3. **ESA WorldCover**: Highest quality free land cover at 10m resolution, updated annually[10][11]

4. **Sentinel-2**: Free 10m multispectral imagery with 5-day revisit for baseline mapping[13]

5. **ERA5 Climate Data**: Most comprehensive climate reanalysis with API access for temperature, precipitation, wind analysis[49][50]

6. **Early Coordination Required**: Protected areas (Environment Authority), archaeological sites (Heritage Ministry), military zones (Defence Ministry), mineral concessions (Energy Ministry) require formal consultation[38][29]

7. **Seismic Hazard**: Critical assessment needed - consult Sultan Qaboos University Earthquake Monitoring Center for site-specific studies[64][63]

8. **Wadi Crossings**: Special attention to ephemeral stream flooding - coordinate with water resources authorities[15][14]

9. **Commercial Imagery**: Budget for Maxar, Airbus, or Planet high-resolution imagery for detailed route surveys and obstacle identification[61][60]

10. **WorldPop/WDPA**: Excellent free global datasets for population density (100m) and protected areas with API access[39][58]
