# GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing in Russia

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### National Sources
**Rosreestr Unified Digital Basemap (UDB)**
- **Source/Provider:** Federal Service for State Registration, Cadastre and Cartography (Rosreestr)[1][2]
- **Resolution/Scale:** 1:10,000 to 1:200,000 (varies by region; 1:25,000 for high population density areas)[2]
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (continuously updated)[2]
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous updates through State Information System[2]
- **Access Type:** Restricted/requires request through official channels[3][1]
- **API Availability:** Yes - OGC-compliant cartographic services available[2]
- **Official URL:** https://rosreestr.gov.ru / http://geoportal.rosreestr.ru/[1]
- **Quality Assessment:** High resolution for developed areas, comprehensive national coverage with legal significance as state cartographic basis[2]

### Global DEMs (Best for Russia)

**Copernicus DEM (Recommended)**
- **Source/Provider:** European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme[4]
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30m (GLO-30) and 90m (GLO-90) resolution[5][4]
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on 2011-2015 data[4]
- **Update Frequency:** Static baseline[4]
- **Access Type:** Open/free[4]
- **API Availability:** Yes - available through Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem[4]
- **Official URL:** https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/
- **Quality Assessment:** Superior accuracy to SRTM (RMSE 2.19-5.72m vs 3.95-6.10m for SRTM); more consistent globally with virtually seamless source[6][7][5]

**SRTM (Shuttle Radar Topography Mission)**
- **Source/Provider:** USGS / NASA[8]
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30m (1 arc-second) and 90m (3 arc-second)[9][8]
- **Temporal Coverage:** February 2000[9]
- **Update Frequency:** Static[8]
- **Access Type:** Open/free[8]
- **API Availability:** Yes - through USGS EarthExplorer API[8]
- **Official URL:** https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov
- **Quality Assessment:** Good baseline coverage for Russia; some voids filled by alternative sources; less accurate than Copernicus DEM[10][9]

**ASTER GDEM**
- **Source/Provider:** USGS / Japan METI[8]
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30m (1 arc-second)[8]
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000-2013[8]
- **Update Frequency:** Static (version 3 released)[8]
- **Access Type:** Open/free[8]
- **API Availability:** Yes - through AppEEARS[8]
- **Official URL:** https://appeears.earthdatacloud.nasa.gov/
- **Quality Assessment:** Generally less accurate than Copernicus DEM and SRTM for Russia; useful as supplementary data[10]

**ESA WorldCover (Recommended)**
- **Source/Provider:** European Space Agency WorldCover Consortium[13][14]
- **Resolution/Scale:** 10m resolution[14][13]

- **Access Type:** Open/free (CC-BY-4.0)[13][14]
- **API Availability:** Yes - Google Earth Engine[16][13]
- **Official URL:** https://esa-worldcover.org

**Google Dynamic World**
- **Source/Provider:** Google / World Resources Institute[17][18]
- **Resolution/Scale:** 10m resolution[18][17]
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present (near real-time)[17]

- **Access Type:** Open/free[18][17]
- **API Availability:** Yes - Google Earth Engine[17][18]
- **Official URL:** https://dynamicworld.app
- **Quality Assessment:** Near real-time updates with per-pixel probabilities across 9 land cover classes; based on Sentinel-2[18][17]

## Key Recommendations for Russia:

1. **Prioritize Copernicus DEM over SRTM** for elevation data due to superior accuracy[6][5]
2. **ESA WorldCover and Dynamic World** provide best free land cover at 10m resolution[14][18][13]
3. **Rosreestr datasets** are most authoritative but require formal access procedures and possibly fees[3][1][2]
4. **Permafrost mapping is critical** - 65% of Russia affected; essential for pipeline foundation design[68][67]
5. **Pipeline infrastructure data** from Gazprom/Transneft is highly restricted; Global Energy Monitor provides strategic-level alternative[34][30]
6. **OpenStreetMap quality is poor** for Russia compared to Western countries; use cautiously and validate against official sources[27][28]
7. **ERA5 climate data** incorporates Russian meteorological stations and provides excellent spatial-temporal coverage[49][47][45]
8. **Commercial satellite imagery** requires licenses but offers 30cm resolution from Maxar/Airbus; Sentinel-2 provides excellent free 10m alternative[63][62][61]
