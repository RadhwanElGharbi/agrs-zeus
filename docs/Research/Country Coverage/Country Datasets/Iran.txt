# GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing in Iran

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### National Products

**National Cartographic Center (NCC) DEM**
- **Source:** National Cartographic Center of Iran (NCC)
- **Resolution:** 1:25,000 and 1:250,000 scale topographic maps converted to DEM
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (project initiated 2001)
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular updates
- **Access:** Restricted/requires formal request from NCC
- **API:** No public API
- **URL:** https://ncc.gov.ir (main portal), https://sdi.mpo-es.ir/en (Iranian National Geoportal)
- **Quality:** High accuracy for Iran; derived from national topographic database with GPS control, but availability is limited and requires government authorization[1][2][3]

**Iranian Remote Sensing Center (IRSC) DEMs**
- **Source:** Iranian Remote Sensing Center
- **Resolution:** 1:25,000 scale (SPOT stereo pairs) for cities; 1:250,000 for country-wide coverage
- **Temporal Coverage:** Generated from 1990s-2010s imagery
- **Update Frequency:** Project-based, not continuously updated
- **Access:** Restricted/requires request
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact IRSC directly (Tehran, Saadat Abad)
- **Quality:** Moderate to high; derived from SPOT satellite stereo imagery and topographic maps with documented generation for major cities[4][3]

### Global Products

**Copernicus DEM (COP-DEM)**
- **Source:** European Space Agency/Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution:** 30m (GLO-30) and 90m (GLO-90)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on 2011-2015 TanDEM-X data
- **Update Frequency:** Static product (no regular updates)
- **Access:** Open/free (registration required for GLO-30)
- **API:** Yes - Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem API
- **URL:** https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM
- **Quality:** Excellent for Iran; RMSE of 5.72m in Tehran, 2.19m in Jam - significantly better than SRTM; most accurate global DEM available[5][6][7]

**SRTM (Shuttle Radar Topography Mission)**
- **Source:** NASA/USGS
- **Resolution:** 30m (SRTM-1) globally available since 2015
- **Temporal Coverage:** February 2000 (single acquisition)
- **Update Frequency:** Static (no updates)
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Yes - USGS Earth Explorer API, Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov
- **Quality:** Good but inferior to Copernicus DEM for Iran; RMSE 6.10m (Tehran), 3.95m (Jam); widely used baseline[8][5]

**ASTER GDEM**
- **Source:** NASA/METI (Japan)
- **Resolution:** 30m
- **Temporal Coverage:** Version 3 (2019) compiled from 2000-2013 images
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic versions (v1: 2009, v2: 2011, v3: 2019)
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Yes - NASA Earthdata API, Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://earthdata.nasa.gov
- **Quality:** Moderate; less accurate than SRTM and Copernicus DEM, particularly in arid regions; prone to artifacts[9][8]

## 2. LAND COVER / LAND USE

### National Products

**Iran Land Cover Map (Sentinel-based)**
- **Source:** Various Iranian research institutions/universities
- **Resolution:** 10m (13-class system)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2017, with updated versions for 2020-2021
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular (research-driven)
- **Access:** Open (via Google Earth Engine and research publications)
- **API:** Yes - Google Earth Engine API
- **URL:** https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/tags/landcover (search "Iran")
- **Quality:** Excellent; specifically calibrated for Iran using Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 data with 10m resolution and improved accuracy over global products[10][11]

**NCC Land Cover Maps**
- **Source:** National Cartographic Center of Iran
- **Resolution:** 1:25,000 scale
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular
- **Access:** Restricted/requires request
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://ncc.gov.ir
- **Quality:** High accuracy where available; official national standard but limited public access[2][12]

### Global Products

**ESA WorldCover**
- **Source:** European Space Agency WorldCover Consortium
- **Resolution:** 10m (11 land cover classes)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2020 and 2021 versions
- **Update Frequency:** Annual (2020, 2021 available)
- **Access:** Open/free (CC-BY-4.0 license)
- **API:** Yes - Google Earth Engine, WMS/WCS services
- **URL:** https://esa-worldcover.org/en and https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/ESA_WorldCover_v200
- **Quality:** Excellent; global validation shows high accuracy, derived from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2; good performance in arid regions like Iran[13][14]

**Google Dynamic World**
- **Source:** Google/World Resources Institute
- **Resolution:** 10m (9 land cover classes)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present (near real-time)
- **Update Frequency:** Near real-time (every Sentinel-2 overpass)
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Yes - Google Earth Engine API only
- **URL:** https://dynamicworld.app
- **Quality:** Very good; unique advantage of temporal tracking; continuous updates allow monitoring of land use changes along pipeline routes[15][16]

**Sentinel-2 10m LULC (ESRI)**
- **Source:** Esri/Impact Observatory
- **Resolution:** 10m
- **Temporal Coverage:** Annual 2017-2023
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Yes - ArcGIS API, Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=cfcb7609de5f478eb7666240902d4d3d
- **Quality:** Very good; annual time series allows change detection; competitive accuracy with ESA WorldCover[17]

## 3. HYDROLOGY / WATER

### National Products

**Iran Ministry of Energy Water Resources Data**
- **Source:** Ministry of Energy (Water Resources Organizations)
- **Resolution:** Basin-scale (30 main hydrological basins)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1986-present with historical records
- **Update Frequency:** Annual reports with monthly/seasonal data
- **Access:** Partially open via Iran Open Data Center; detailed data requires request
- **API:** No standardized API
- **URL:** https://iranopendata.org/en (search "water" or "dam")
- **Quality:** Official government data; comprehensive basin-level statistics on surface water, groundwater, and discharge; most authoritative for Iran[18][19][20]

**Iran National Rivers/Wadis Database**
- **Source:** Department of Environment (DOE) / Ministry of Energy
- **Resolution:** Varies by basin (sub-basin level detail)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular
- **Access:** Restricted/requires formal request
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact Ministry of Energy or DOE directly
- **Quality:** High for major water bodies; comprehensive coverage of 30 main basins including seasonal wadis important for pipeline routing[18]

### Global Products

**HydroSHEDS / HydroBASINS**
- **Source:** WWF/USGS/McGill University
- **Resolution:** 15 arc-second (~450m) base resolution; HydroBASINS at multiple scales
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on 2000 SRTM data
- **Update Frequency:** Static (occasional version updates)
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Limited (downloadable datasets)
- **URL:** https://www.hydrosheds.org
- **Quality:** Very good for Iran; standardized global hydrographic network with nested sub-basin hierarchy; excellent for catchment analysis[21][22][23]

**Global Surface Water Explorer (Copernicus)**
- **Source:** European Commission Joint Research Centre
- **Resolution:** 30m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1984-present (Landsat archive)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual updates
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Yes - Google Earth Engine API
- **URL:** https://global-surface-water.appspot.com
- **Quality:** Excellent; shows water occurrence, seasonality, and change over 35+ years; critical for understanding ephemeral water bodies in Iran[24]

**Hydrography90m**
- **Source:** IGB Berlin/Yale University
- **Resolution:** 90m globally consistent
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on MERIT DEM (2017)
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Limited
- **URL:** Via HydroSHEDS portal
- **Quality:** Very good; higher resolution than original HydroSHEDS with improved stream network delineation[25]

## 8. PROTECTED AREAS / ENVIRONMENTAL

### National Products

**Iran Department of Environment (DOE) Protected Areas**
- **Source:** Department of Environment, Islamic Republic of Iran
- **Resolution:** 1:50,000 scale mapping
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2017 compilation
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic updates
- **Access:** Restricted/requires request from DOE
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact DOE directly
- **Quality:** Most authoritative; includes national parks, wildlife refuges, protected areas, and wildlife parks; comprehensive three-tier protection system established since 1967[30][31]

### Global Products

**World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA)**
- **Source:** UNEP-WCMC/IUCN
- **Resolution:** Vector polygons of protected area boundaries
- **Temporal Coverage:** Continuously updated (monthly releases)
- **Update Frequency:** Monthly
- **Access:** Open/free (non-commercial use)
- **API:** Yes - Protected Planet API
- **URL:** https://www.protectedplanet.net/en/country/IRN
- **Quality:** Good; includes Iran's protected areas with international categorization; approximately 11% of Iran covered; derived from official sources but may lag national updates; essential for international-standard conservation constraints[32][33][30]

**UNESCO World Heritage Sites**
- **Source:** UNESCO World Heritage Centre
- **Resolution:** Site boundaries (vector)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (26 sites in Iran as of 2025)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual World Heritage Committee meetings
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Limited GIS data via UNESCO Sites Navigator
- **URL:** https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/ir
- **Quality:** Authoritative for cultural heritage sites; includes Sassanid archaeological landscapes and other culturally sensitive areas requiring pipeline avoidance[34][35]

## 9. GEOLOGY / SOIL

### National Products

**Geological Survey of Iran (GSIr) Maps**
- **Source:** Geological Survey of Iran (founded 1958)
- **Resolution:** 1:250,000 and 1:100,000 scale geological maps
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1970s-present (ongoing coverage)
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular; publication program resumed in 1990s
- **Access:** Restricted/requires request; some maps available for purchase
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact GSIr directly (https://www.gsi.ir/en if available)
- **Quality:** Excellent; most comprehensive geological mapping of Iran; 1:250,000 covers entire country with explanatory texts; 1:100,000 coverage extensive; includes aeromagnetic surveys; some sheets published, others completed but not released[36][1]

**National Iranian Oil Company (NIOC) Geological Maps**
- **Source:** National Iranian Oil Company Exploration Division
- **Resolution:** 1:1,000,000 scale
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1975-1978 for national coverage
- **Update Frequency:** Historic (completed series)
- **Access:** Restricted
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Not publicly accessible
- **Quality:** High for regional geology; complete 1:1M coverage produced for oil exploration; magnetic intensity maps also available[36][1]

**FAO/Iran Soil Maps**
- **Source:** FAO with Iranian collaborators (Dewan & Famouri)
- **Resolution:** Various scales (generalized national maps)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1970s-1980s compilation
- **Update Frequency:** Static (historical legacy maps)
- **Access:** Open via FAO Soil Portal
- **API:** No standardized API
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/soils-portal/data-hub/soil-maps-and-databases/fao-soil-legacy-maps/en/
- **Quality:** Moderate; dated but useful for regional soil classification; superseded by global products for most applications[37][38][39]

### Global Products

**FAO Harmonized World Soil Database (HWSD v2.0)**
- **Source:** FAO/IIASA/ISRIC
- **Resolution:** 1km raster
- **Temporal Coverage:** Version 2.0 released 2023 (comprehensive update)
- **Update Frequency:** Major versions (v1.0: 2008, v1.2: 2013, v2.0: 2023)
- **Access:** Open/free (registration required)
- **API:** No (download-based)
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/soils-portal/data-hub/soil-maps-and-databases/harmonized-world-soil-database-v20/en/
- **Quality:** Very good; most comprehensive global soil database; v2.0 includes 7 soil layers with detailed morphology, chemistry, and physical characteristics; best global option for Iran[40][41]

**OneGeology Portal**
- **Source:** Global geological surveys consortium
- **Resolution:** Variable (1:1M to 1:250,000 depending on country)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Compilation of national surveys
- **Update Frequency:** As national surveys contribute data
- **Access:** Open/free (view and download)
- **API:** Yes - WMS/WFS services
- **URL:** http://portal.onegeology.org
- **Quality:** Good; aggregates geological survey data globally; Iran coverage depends on GSIr contributions; useful for regional context[1]

**USGS Surficial Geology of Iran**
- **Source:** USGS (compiled from various sources)
- **Resolution:** National-scale compilation
- **Temporal Coverage:** Published 2021
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Via USGS APIs
- **URL:** https://data.usgs.gov/datacatalog/data/USGS:60ad3db2d34e4043c850f2a0
- **Quality:** Good for general overview; includes arcs, polygons, and labels describing geologic age and bedrock types[42][43]

## 10. CLIMATE / WEATHER

### National Products

**Iran Meteorological Organization (IRIMO)**
- **Source:** Islamic Republic of Iran Meteorological Organization
- **Resolution:** Station-based network (103+ synoptic stations)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical records from 1950s-present; real-time observations
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time (hourly/3-hourly observations); daily forecasts
- **Access:** Partially open; detailed historical data requires request
- **API:** No official public API
- **URL:** https://www.irimo.ir/index.php?newlang=eng
- **Quality:** Most authoritative for Iran; comprehensive network covering all 30 hydrological basins; includes temperature, precipitation, wind data; also provides agrometeorological information (TAHAK system)[44][45][46][47][18]

### Global Products

**ERA5 Climate Reanalysis**
- **Source:** ECMWF/Copernicus Climate Change Service
- **Resolution:** 31km (0.25° grid); hourly temporal resolution
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1940-present (3-month lag for real-time)
- **Update Frequency:** Daily updates (with lag)
- **Access:** Open/free (registration required)
- **API:** Yes - Climate Data Store (CDS) API
- **URL:** https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu
- **Quality:** Excellent; most comprehensive global reanalysis; provides temperature, precipitation, wind, pressure, humidity, solar radiation; 137 vertical levels; best global climate dataset for Iran; validated extensively[48][49][50][51][52][53]

**WorldClim**
- **Source:** WorldClim.org (University of California, Berkeley)
- **Resolution:** 30 arc-second (~1km) to 30 arc-minute
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical climate 1970-2000; future projections to 2100
- **Update Frequency:** Major versions (current: WorldClim 2.1)
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** No (direct download)
- **URL:** https://www.worldclim.org
- **Quality:** Very good; provides bioclimatic variables, monthly temperature/precipitation; widely used for ecological modeling; suitable for long-term climate characterization[44]

## 12. POPULATION / DEMOGRAPHICS

### National Products

**Statistical Center of Iran Census Data**
- **Source:** Statistical Center of Iran
- **Resolution:** Administrative unit level (province, county, district)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Census years (most recent available: 2016)
- **Update Frequency:** ~10-year census cycles
- **Access:** Restricted/requires request; some aggregate data published
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact Statistical Center of Iran
- **Quality:** Most authoritative; official population counts by administrative unit; detailed demographic characteristics[56]

### Global Products

**WorldPop**
- **Source:** University of Southampton/WorldPop Project
- **Resolution:** 100m (3 arc-second) resolution
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000-2020 (annual)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual releases
- **Access:** Open/free (CC-BY 4.0)
- **API:** No (FTP download)
- **URL:** https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/summary?id=41767 (Iran specific)
- **Quality:** Excellent; high-resolution population density using Random Forest dasymetric redistribution; widely validated; best openly available gridded population for Iran; units are persons per square kilometer[57][58][59]

**LandScan Global**
- **Source:** Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL)
- **Resolution:** 1km (30 arc-second)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Annual products (2000-present)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access:** Free registration required; some restrictions on redistribution
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://landscan.ornl.gov
- **Quality:** Very good; ambient population distribution (24-hour average); incorporates infrastructure, terrain, land cover; complementary to WorldPop; widely used by US government and international organizations[55]

**GPWv4 (Gridded Population of the World)**
- **Source:** CIESIN, Columbia University
- **Resolution:** 30 arc-second (~1km)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000-2020 (5-year intervals)
- **Update Frequency:** Major versions every 5-10 years
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Limited (download-based)
- **URL:** https://sedac.ciesin.columbia.edu/data/collection/gpw-v4
- **Quality:** Good; simpler redistribution method than WorldPop but based on official census data; provides growth rates and projection datasets[56]

- **Access:** Commercial/purchase required

**Planet Labs**
- **Source:** Planet Labs

- **Access:** Commercial/subscription or purchase
- **API:** Yes - Planet API
- **URL:** https://www.planet.com
- **Quality:** Very good; unique advantage is daily revisit for monitoring; SkySat provides sub-meter resolution; ideal for change detection along pipeline routes[61][62]

**Capella Space (SAR)**
- **Source:** Capella Space
- **Resolution:** 25cm X-band SAR
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2020-present
- **Update Frequency:** On-demand tasking
- **Access:** Commercial/purchase required
- **API:** Yes - Capella API
- **URL:** https://www.capellaspace.com

### Free Baseline Imagery

**Sentinel-2**
- **Source:** European Space Agency/Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution:** 10m (visible/NIR), 20m (red edge/SWIR), 60m (coastal/atmospheric)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present
- **Update Frequency:** 5-day revisit (2 satellites)
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Yes - Copernicus Open Access Hub API, Google Earth Engine, AWS Open Data
- **URL:** https://scihub.copernicus.eu or https://dataspace.copernicus.eu
- **Quality:** Excellent for free data; 13 spectral bands; systematic global coverage; sufficient for most land cover and change detection applications; essential free baseline for Iran[65][13][10]

**Landsat 8/9**
- **Source:** NASA/USGS
- **Resolution:** 30m (multispectral), 15m (panchromatic), 100m (thermal)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Landsat archive from 1972-present (Landsat 8: 2013-, Landsat 9: 2021-)
- **Update Frequency:** 16-day revisit (combined 8-day with both satellites)
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Yes - USGS Earth Explorer API, Google Earth Engine, AWS Open Data
- **URL:** https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov
- **Quality:** Very good; longest-running Earth observation program; thermal bands valuable for infrastructure monitoring; excellent for historical analysis[66][4]

- **Source:** Building and Housing Research Center (BHRC) / Iranian code authority
- **Resolution:** National map with 4 hazard zones (0.35g, 0.30g, 0.25g, 0.20g)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Updated 2012-2014 (4th edition)
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular (as seismic code updates)
- **Access:** Included in Standard 2800; available through BHRC
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.dlubal.com/en/load-zones-for-snow-wind-earthquake/seismic-ir-2800.html (visualization)
- **Quality:** Excellent; official seismic design standard for Iran; includes GIS layers of faults, epicenters, strong motion records; critical for pipeline routing in one of world's most seismically active regions; ~70% of Iran in high hazard zone[67][68][69]

**Iran Seismotectonic Province Maps**
- **Source:** Various Iranian research institutions / IIEES
- **Resolution:** 19 seismotectonic provinces with detailed parameters
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current seismic characterization
- **Update Frequency:** Research-driven updates
- **Access:** Via scientific publications and IIEES
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.iiees.ac.ir/en/
- **Quality:** Very good; includes b-values, Mmax, seismogenic depth, Moho depth for each province; essential for probabilistic seismic hazard assessment[70][71][67]

**Updated Fault Map of Iran**
- **Source:** International Institute of Earthquake Engineering and Seismology (IIEES)
- **Resolution:** National coverage with digitized active faults
- **Temporal Coverage:** Updated 2011
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic updates
- **Access:** Available from IIEES
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.iiees.ac.ir/en/updated-fault-map-of-iran/
- **Quality:** Excellent; most comprehensive active fault database for Iran with digital files; essential for pipeline route avoidance of active faults[72]

### Archaeological/Cultural Heritage

**Iran Cultural Heritage Organization Sites Database**
- **Source:** Iranian Cultural Heritage, Handicrafts and Tourism Organization (ICHTO)
- **Resolution:** Individual site locations
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Ongoing as sites discovered/registered
- **Access:** Restricted; some data via UNESCO for World Heritage Sites
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact ICHTO directly
- **Quality:** Most authoritative for Iran; includes registered archaeological sites, historical monuments, and culturally sensitive areas requiring avoidance[73][34]

**NextGIS Cultural Heritage Dataset - Iran**
- **Source:** Wikimedia Foundation/OpenStreetMap compilation
- **Resolution:** Point locations
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Community-driven
- **Access:** Open/free
- **API:** Limited
- **URL:** https://data.nextgis.com/en/region/IR/heritage/
- **Quality:** Moderate; useful for preliminary screening but incomplete compared to official ICHTO database; suitable for identifying major known sites[74]

**Archaeological Predictive Models**
- **Source:** Various research institutions
- **Resolution:** Site-specific or regional studies
- **Temporal Coverage:** Research-driven
- **Update Frequency:** As studies published
- **Access:** Via scientific publications
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Research databases
- **Quality:** Variable; GIS-based predictive modeling of archaeological site locations (e.g., northeastern Iran studies); useful for identifying high-probability areas requiring field survey[75][73]

### Landslide/Geohazard

**Iran Landslide Inventory**
- **Source:** Geological Survey of Iran / research institutions
- **Resolution:** Individual landslide mapping at various scales
- **Temporal Coverage:** Compilation of historical landslides
- **Update Frequency:** Event-driven updates
- **Access:** Restricted/requires request from GSIr
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact GSIr
- **Quality:** Good where available; important for mountainous regions (Zagros, Alborz); essential for pipeline route risk assessment[67]

### Military/Restricted Zones

**Iran Military/Security Zones**
- **Source:** Various government agencies (classified)
- **Resolution:** Zone boundaries
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** As designations change
- **Access:** Not publicly available (classified)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Not accessible
- **Quality:** Authoritative but unavailable; must be obtained through official channels during formal project approvals; critical constraint for pipeline routing[1]

### Drought/Climate Risk

**Iran Drought Monitoring System**
- **Source:** IRIMO/Iran Water Resources Management Company
- **Resolution:** Basin-scale monitoring
- **Temporal Coverage:** Ongoing monitoring (historical records available)
- **Update Frequency:** Seasonal/monthly updates
- **Access:** Partially available via IRIMO
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Via IRIMO portal
- **Quality:** Good; critical for understanding water stress and environmental constraints; Iran faces severe drought conditions affecting ~70% of territory[47][18]

## IMPORTANT NOTES FOR IRAN:

**Access Challenges:** Iran faces significant data access restrictions due to:
- International sanctions affecting commercial satellite imagery access
- National security concerns around infrastructure data
- Limited public data sharing culture in government agencies
- Geopolitical sensitivities

**Recommendations:**
1. **Prioritize global open datasets** (Copernicus DEM, ESA WorldCover, ERA5, WDPA, WorldPop) which provide excellent coverage without access restrictions
2. **Commercial imagery** from Maxar and Airbus is available but may require careful compliance review
3. **National datasets** from NCC, GSIr, IRIMO, and DOE provide highest accuracy but require formal requests through Iranian government channels
4. **OpenStreetMap quality** is moderate - suitable for preliminary work but requires verification
5. **Critical infrastructure data** (pipelines, power, military zones) is effectively unavailable publicly due to security classification
6. For **seismic hazard assessment**, prioritize Iranian Standard 2800 maps and IIEES fault databases - these are essential given Iran's high seismicity
7. The **Iranian National Geoportal** (sdi.mpo-es.ir/en) is the primary access point for requesting national datasets

**API Access Summary:** Best API availability through:
- Copernicus services (DEM, Sentinel-2, ERA5)
- Google Earth Engine (multiple global datasets)
- NASA Earthdata
- Protected Planet (WDPA)
- Limited or no API for Iranian national datasets

This comprehensive assessment reflects data availability as of October 2025, with emphasis on openly accessible global datasets supplemented by information about restricted national sources that require formal access procedures.
