# GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing in Norway

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### National High-Resolution DEM (NDH DTM1)
- **Source:** Kartverket (Norwegian Mapping Authority)
- **Resolution:** 1m × 1m (LiDAR-derived)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2016–present (ongoing acquisition)
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous (project covers most of Norway)
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - WMS/WFS services via Geonorge
- **URL:** https://www.kartverket.no/en/api-and-data, https://www.geonorge.no
- **Quality:** Excellent - highest resolution national DEM, LiDAR-based terrain model with vertical reference NN2000 (EVRF2007 compatible)[1][2][3]

### National DEM 10m (DTM10/DOM10)
- **Source:** Kartverket
- **Resolution:** 10m × 10m
- **Temporal Coverage:** Complete coverage (2018 version widely distributed)
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic updates
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - WMS/WFS via Geonorge
- **URL:** https://www.geonorge.no
- **Quality:** Very Good - complete nationwide coverage, LiDAR and photogrammetry-derived, suitable for regional analysis[2][3]

### Copernicus DEM (Best Global Option)
- **Source:** European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution:** 30m (GLO-30) and 90m (GLO-90)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2011–2015 (TanDEM-X mission data)
- **Update Frequency:** Static baseline
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - various download services
- **URL:** https://land.copernicus.eu
- **Quality:** Good - global consistency, lower resolution than national products but complete coverage including offshore contexts[4][5]

## 2. LAND COVER / LAND USE

### AR-STAT Land Resource Map
- **Source:** NIBIO (Norwegian Institute of Bioeconomy Research) / Statistics Norway (SSB)
- **Resolution:** High-resolution vector (cadastral parcel level)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Annual updates
- **Update Frequency:** Annually
- **Access:** Open/Free via Geonorge
- **API:** Yes - SSB API and Geonorge services
- **URL:** https://www.ssb.no/en/natur-og-miljo/areal/statistikk/arealbruk-og-arealressurser, https://data.ssb.no/api
- **Quality:** Excellent - hierarchical classification system, integrates cadastral data, building footprints from FKB, road networks, highly detailed for Norway[6][7][8]

### National Land Use Statistics
- **Source:** Statistics Norway (SSB)
- **Resolution:** Vector - parcel and cadastral level
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Annually
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - SSB has ~200 ready-made datasets in JSON-stat and CSV
- **URL:** https://data.ssb.no/api, https://www.geonorge.no
- **Quality:** Very Good - official statistics, harmonized with European standards, integrates multiple authoritative sources[7][8]

### ESA WorldCover (Best Global)
- **Source:** European Space Agency
- **Resolution:** 10m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2020–2021
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes
- **URL:** https://worldcover.esa.int
- **Quality:** Very Good - Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 based, 11 land cover classes, validated globally[5]

### Copernicus Global Land Cover
- **Source:** Copernicus Land Monitoring Service
- **Resolution:** 10m (enhanced version)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015–present
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes
- **URL:** https://land.copernicus.eu/en/products/global-dynamic-land-cover
- **Quality:** Very Good - enhanced spatial detail over previous 100m versions[5]

## 3. HYDROLOGY / WATER

### National Hydrological Database (Hydra II)
- **Source:** NVE (Norwegian Water Resources and Energy Directorate)
- **Resolution:** Station-based time series and catchment networks
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical records (some >100 years) to present
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time for many stations
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Likely available (contact NVE)
- **URL:** https://www.nve.no/hydrology
- **Quality:** Excellent - national reference dataset for climate studies, includes streamflow, groundwater, snow, glacier data, long-term quality-controlled records[9][10]

### National River Network Database (REGINE)
- **Source:** NVE
- **Resolution:** Vector - detailed river segments, streams, lakes
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic updates
- **Access:** Open/Free via Geonorge
- **API:** Yes - INSPIRE-compliant WFS
- **URL:** https://www.geonorge.no, https://inspire-geoportal.ec.europa.eu
- **Quality:** Excellent - complete coverage of watercourses including coastal fields, all elements (rivers, streams, lakes) geo-referenced[11][12]

### Vann-Nett (Water Management Database)
- **Source:** Miljødirektoratet / NVE
- **Resolution:** Waterbody polygons and networks
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Regular updates for WFD compliance
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Via Geonorge services
- **URL:** https://www.miljodirektoratet.no, https://www.geonorge.no
- **Quality:** Very Good - EU Water Framework Directive compliant, ecological condition assessments included[13][12]

### Global Surface Water Explorer (Best Global)
- **Source:** European Commission Joint Research Centre
- **Resolution:** 30m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1984–present (Landsat-based)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://global-surface-water.appspot.com
- **Quality:** Good - long-term temporal coverage, seasonal variations captured

## 4. INFRASTRUCTURE - ROADS

### National Road Database (NVDB)
- **Source:** Statens vegvesen (Norwegian Public Roads Administration)
- **Resolution:** Very detailed vector - centerline and regulation data
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Continuously updated
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - NVDB API v3 (XML/JSON)
- **URL:** https://dataut.vegvesen.no/en/dataset/vegnett-og-vegregulering-i-nvdb, http://nvdbtransportportal.vegdata.no
- **Quality:** Excellent - complete coverage of public, private, forest roads; includes attributes like height restrictions, road width, bridges, tunnels, speed limits[14][15][16]

### OpenStreetMap Norway
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap contributors
- **Resolution:** Variable vector data
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous community updates
- **Access:** Open/Free (ODbL license)
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org
- **Quality:** Very Good - Norway is among the most complete OSM countries globally (>97% estimated road completeness), though POI data is less complete; Denmark/Norway/Iceland considered top-tier OSM coverage[17][18][19]

### Commercial Providers
- **HERE Technologies:** High-precision road data, commercial license required
- **TomTom:** Commercial navigation-grade data, license required
- **Quality:** Excellent but expensive; unnecessary given high-quality open Norwegian data

### National Pipeline Registry

- **Resolution:** Unknown
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current

- **Access:** Restricted/requires formal request
- **API:** No

## 10. CLIMATE / WEATHER

### MET Norway Weather API
- **Source:** MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute)
- **Resolution:** 1 km (MET Nordic model), station data, various products
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical archives, real-time, forecasts
- **Update Frequency:** Hourly updates for forecasts, continuous for observations
- **Access:** Open/Free (CC BY 4.0 / NLOD license)
- **API:** Yes - comprehensive API (api.met.no) with multiple services
- **URL:** https://api.met.no, https://frost.met.no (historical/climate data)
- **Quality:** Excellent - authoritative national source, 1 km resolution weather model for North Europe, comprehensive historical climate data via Frost API, real-time observations, satellite data[33][34][35][36][37]

### THREDDS Data Server
- **Source:** MET Norway
- **Resolution:** Gridded atmospheric, ocean, wave data
- **Temporal Coverage:** Various
- **Update Frequency:** Regular
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - OPeNDAP, NetCDF format
- **URL:** https://thredds.met.no
- **Quality:** Excellent - full-field data across larger areas[36]

### ERA5 Reanalysis (Best Global Historical)
- **Source:** ECMWF / Copernicus Climate Change Service
- **Resolution:** ~31 km (0.25°)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1940–present
- **Update Frequency:** Near real-time (5-day lag)
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Climate Data Store API
- **URL:** https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu
- **Quality:** Excellent - global coverage, hourly temporal resolution, comprehensive atmospheric variables

### WorldClim (Global Climatology)
- **Source:** WorldClim.org
- **Resolution:** 1 km (30 arc-seconds)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1970–2000 baseline, future projections available
- **Update Frequency:** Static climatologies
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** No - direct download
- **URL:** https://www.worldclim.org
- **Quality:** Very Good - standard for species distribution modeling and climate analysis

## Key Access Portals

**Geonorge** (https://www.geonorge.no) is Norway's central geospatial data portal and should be the first stop for all national datasets. It provides integrated access to data from Kartverket, NGU, NVE, Miljødirektoratet, SSB, and other agencies with standardized metadata, WMS/WFS APIs, and bulk download options.[51][52][53]

**Norway Digital** is the national SDI framework coordinating data sharing across agencies, ensuring INSPIRE compliance and interoperability.[39][54][38]

For pipeline routing in Norway, prioritize the exceptional 1m LiDAR elevation data (NDH DTM1), official road/railway networks, NVE hydrological data, landslide/quick clay hazard maps, and protected areas from Miljødirektoratet. The quality and completeness of Norwegian national datasets far exceed global alternatives.[1][45][25]
