# GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing in Iraq

Based on comprehensive research, Iraq presents significant data challenges compared to other oil-producing nations due to limited open government datasets. The OGIM database assessment indicates Iraq has one of the lowest data quality scores globally for oil and gas infrastructure (<0.3), with most data from non-government sources. Here are the best available datasets:[1][2]

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### **Copernicus DEM GLO-30** (HIGHEST QUALITY)
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2011-2015 (TanDEM-X mission)
- **Update Frequency:** Static (maintained until 2026)
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem API
- **URL:** https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/ and https://portal.opentopography.org/raster?opentopoID=OTSDEM.032021.4326.3
- **Quality Assessment:** RMSE of 1.3m in Iraq - 2.6x more accurate than SRTM and 5.2x more accurate than ASTER GDEM. Best global DEM for Iraq[3][4][5]

### **NASA SRTM (30m and 90m)**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30m (SRTM-1), 90m (SRTM-3)
- **Temporal Coverage:** February 2000
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - USGS Earth Explorer API
- **URL:** https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov
- **Quality Assessment:** Vertical accuracy ±8-10m for Iraq region. Widely used baseline but inferior to Copernicus DEM[6][3]

### **ASTER GDEM v3**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000-2013
- **Update Frequency:** Static (v3 released 2019)
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - via EarthData API
- **URL:** https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov
- **Quality Assessment:** Lowest accuracy among major DEMs for Iraq (RMSE ~6.8m), contains artifacts in flat areas[7][3]

### **ALOS World 3D (AW3D30)**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2006-2011
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Free/Open (registration required)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.eorc.jaxa.jp/ALOS/en/aw3d30/
- **Quality Assessment:** Good accuracy, better than ASTER but slightly inferior to Copernicus DEM for Middle East[3][7]

**National Datasets:** Directorate General of Geological Surveys and Mineral Investigation (DGGSMI) maintains geological maps, but no publicly accessible national LiDAR or high-resolution DEM datasets were identified.[8][9]

## 2. LAND COVER / LAND USE

### **Kurdistan Region Land Cover Map 2024**
- **Source:** Kurdistan Region Statistics Office (KRSO) with World Food Programme support
- **Resolution/Scale:** Based on Sentinel-2 (10m), produced at higher resolution using machine learning
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2023-2024
- **Update Frequency:** Annual (planned)
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Request (government agency)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact KRSO directly
- **Quality Assessment:** High-quality supervised classification for Kurdistan Region only. Field-verified. First such work in Iraq. Federal government requested national extension[10]

### **ESA WorldCover 10m**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 10m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2020, 2021 (annual updates)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - via Copernicus API
- **URL:** https://viewer.esa-worldcover.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Global validation accuracy 74.4%. Best global land cover product for Iraq. Excellent for identifying vegetation, cropland, built-up areas, bare ground[10]

### **Google Dynamic World**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 10m (Sentinel-2 based)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present
- **Update Frequency:** Near real-time (every 2-5 days)
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://www.dynamicworld.app/
- **Quality Assessment:** Near real-time capability valuable for monitoring land use changes. 8 land cover classes[10]

### **Sentinel-2 Land Cover Classification**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 10m-20m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous (5-day revisit)
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - Copernicus Open Access Hub API
- **URL:** https://scihub.copernicus.eu/
- **Quality Assessment:** Raw imagery requires processing but provides most current data[10]

**National Datasets:** No comprehensive national land use database publicly available from Iraqi ministries.

## 3. HYDROLOGY / WATER

### **Iraq Ministry of Water Resources Database**
- **Source:** Ministry of Water Resources of Republic of Iraq
- **Resolution/Scale:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical to current
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Request
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact ministry directly
- **Quality Assessment:** Most authoritative for Iraq but access severely limited. Contains hydrological, climatic, water quality data[11][12][13]

### **HydroSHEDS (Hydrological data and maps based on SHuttle Elevation Derivatives at multiple Scales)**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 15 arc-seconds (~500m at equator)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on SRTM 2000
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.hydrosheds.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Good for river network delineation, watershed boundaries. Based on older SRTM data[11]

### **Global Surface Water Explorer (Copernicus/JRC)**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30m
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1984-2022
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/
- **Quality Assessment:** Excellent for identifying permanent/seasonal water bodies, wetlands. Critical for Iraq's marshlands and wadis[11]

### **OpenStreetMap Water Features**
- **Resolution/Scale:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous (crowdsourced)
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Incomplete for Iraq. Tigris and Euphrates rivers mapped but many wadis and smaller features missing[14][11]

**Quality Note:** Iraq has complex hydrogeology with 7 hydrogeological zones, extensive karst aquifers in northeast, and shared transboundary aquifer systems.[11]

## 4. INFRASTRUCTURE - ROADS

### **OpenStreetMap Iraq Roads**
- **Source:** Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT)
- **Resolution/Scale:** Variable, typically 1-5m accuracy
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (continuously updated)
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time (crowdsourced)
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API
- **URL:** https://data.humdata.org/dataset/hotosm_irq_roads
- **Quality Assessment:** Moderate quality. Studies show substantial horizontal positional discrepancies (RMSE ~6m) compared to official Baghdad data. Better coverage in urban areas. Kurdistan Region has better coverage than southern Iraq[15][16][17][14]

### **Iraq Ministry of Transport Road Network**
- **Source:** Ministry of Transport / Mayoralty of Baghdad (for Baghdad)
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Not publicly available
- **API:** No
- **Quality Assessment:** Most accurate for Iraq but not openly accessible[15]

### **HERE Technologies**
- **Resolution/Scale:** Sub-meter to 5m accuracy
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Quarterly
- **Access Type:** Commercial/Purchase
- **API:** Yes - HERE Location Services API
- **URL:** https://www.here.com/
- **Quality Assessment:** High accuracy, includes attributes (road class, surface type, speed limits). Iraq coverage available but expensive[15]

### **TomTom**
- **Resolution/Scale:** Sub-meter to 5m accuracy
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Quarterly
- **Access Type:** Commercial/Purchase
- **API:** Yes - TomTom Maps API
- **URL:** https://www.tomtom.com/
- **Quality Assessment:** Similar quality to HERE. Includes traffic data. Commercial license required[15]

## 5. INFRASTRUCTURE - RAILWAYS

### **OpenStreetMap Railways Iraq**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap contributors
- **Resolution/Scale:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/ and https://data.humdata.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Incomplete. Major rail lines between Baghdad, Basra, and Mosul mapped but many secondary lines and railway facilities missing[18][14]

### **Iraq National Railway Authority Database**
- **Access Type:** Not publicly available
- **Quality Assessment:** Official data exists but not accessible to public[14]

## 6. INFRASTRUCTURE - UTILITIES (Power, Telecom)

### **OpenInfraMap / OpenStreetMap Utilities**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap
- **Resolution/Scale:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API
- **URL:** https://openinframap.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Very incomplete for Iraq. Major power transmission lines partially mapped but distribution networks largely absent. Telecom infrastructure mostly unmapped[18][14]

### **Iraq Ministry of Electricity Data**
- **Access Type:** Not publicly available
- **Quality Assessment:** Official transmission network data exists but restricted access

## 7. INFRASTRUCTURE - PIPELINES

### **OGIM Database (Oil and Gas Infrastructure Mapping)**
- **Source:** Environmental Defense Fund / Stanford University
- **Resolution/Scale:** Point and line features
- **Temporal Coverage:** Compiled through 2022
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular updates
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7466757
- **Quality Assessment:** Iraq has very limited data in OGIM (data quality score <0.3). Mostly non-government sources. Incomplete coverage. Better than nothing but significant gaps[2][1]

### **Global Oil Infrastructure Tracker (Global Energy Monitor)**
- **Source:** Global Energy Monitor
- **Resolution/Scale:** Line features for pipelines
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Quarterly
- **Access Type:** Free/Open (registration may be required)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://globalenergymonitor.org/projects/global-oil-infrastructure-tracker/
- **Quality Assessment:** Limited coverage for Iraq. Focuses on major transmission pipelines[19]

### **OpenStreetMap Pipelines**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap
- **Resolution/Scale:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Very incomplete for Iraq. Security sensitivities limit public mapping[14][18]

### **Iraq National Oil Companies (SOC, NOC, KRG Ministry)**
- **Access Type:** Highly restricted/Not publicly available
- **Quality Assessment:** South Oil Company, North Oil Company, and Kurdistan Regional Government Ministry of Natural Resources maintain detailed GIS databases but these are confidential for security and commercial reasons[1][2]

### **Commercial Providers (Bayphase, IHS Markit)**
- **Source:** Bayphase Data, IHS Markit, Wood Mackenzie
- **Access Type:** Commercial/Purchase (expensive)
- **API:** Varies by provider
- **URL:** https://www.bayphasedata.com/
- **Quality Assessment:** Most comprehensive pipeline data for Iraq available commercially. Includes field locations, well data, pipeline routes, facilities. Very expensive licensing[20]

## 8. PROTECTED AREAS / ENVIRONMENTAL

### **World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA)**
- **Source:** UNEP-WCMC
- **Resolution/Scale:** Polygon and point features
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (as of May 2021, 23 PAs; updated September 2025)
- **Update Frequency:** Monthly
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - via Protected Planet API
- **URL:** https://www.protectedplanet.net/country/IRQ
- **Quality Assessment:** Iraq has 23 protected areas (1.5% terrestrial coverage, 6,713.7 km²). 16 additional proposed PAs not included. Coverage includes Central Marshes (Ramsar site, UNESCO World Heritage), Al-Hammar Marsh, Hawizeh Marsh, Sawa Lake. Data quality good but limited spatial extent[21][22][23][24]

### **Iraq Key Biodiversity Areas**
- **Source:** Nature Iraq (2017 report)
- **Resolution/Scale:** Site-specific polygons
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2017
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Report-based/Request from Nature Iraq
- **API:** No
- **Quality Assessment:** 82 Key Biodiversity Areas identified covering 6.5% of country (28,388 km²). Includes habitat maps and conservation recommendations. Valuable supplement to WDPA[21]

### **Iraq Ministry of Environment / State Board of Antiquities and Heritage**
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Request
- **Quality Assessment:** Iraq government notes 5 sites formally designated, 18 in ratification process. Direct contact required for official boundaries[21]

## 9. GEOLOGY / SOIL

### **Directorate General of Geological Surveys and Mineral Investigation (DGGSMI)**
- **Source:** Iraqi government
- **Resolution/Scale:** 1:1,000,000 scale geological map
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Request
- **API:** No
- **Quality Assessment:** Most authoritative for Iraq. Small-scale geological mapping exists but digital access limited. Paper maps being digitized[9][25][8]

### **USGS Iraq Geologic/Petroleum Provinces**
- **Source:** U.S. Geological Survey
- **Resolution/Scale:** 1:5,000,000 regional scale
- **Temporal Coverage:** Based on multiple sources
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/?tags=republic-of-iraq
- **Quality Assessment:** Regional overview quality. Useful for petroleum provinces but too coarse for detailed pipeline routing[26][27]

### **OneGeology - Iraq**
- **Source:** OneGeology portal
- **Resolution/Scale:** 1:1,000,000
- **Temporal Coverage:** Various
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - WMS/WFS services
- **URL:** http://www.onegeology.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Provides access to Iraqi geological data through international portal but coverage is limited[25]

### **FAO Harmonized World Soil Database (HWSD) v2.0**
- **Source:** FAO, IIASA, ISRIC
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30 arc-seconds (~1km)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Composite (various dates)
- **Update Frequency:** Major updates (v2.0 released 2023)
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - available through Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/soils-portal/data-hub/ and https://gee-community-catalog.org/projects/hwsd/
- **Quality Assessment:** Global coverage including Iraq. Includes soil organic carbon, pH, water storage capacity, soil depth, texture. Best available global soil dataset. Seven depth layers in v2.0[28][29][30]

### **FAO-UNESCO Soil Map of the World**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 1:5,000,000
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1961-1981
- **Update Frequency:** Static (historical)
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/soils-portal/data-hub/soil-maps-and-databases/faounesco-soil-map-of-the-world/en/
- **Quality Assessment:** Historical baseline but coarse resolution. Iraq included in South Asia volume[31][32]

### **JRC Soil Resources - Mediterranean Countries**
- **Source:** European Commission Joint Research Centre
- **Resolution/Scale:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2013
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/
- **Quality Assessment:** Includes Iraq in Mediterranean/Caucasus assessment. Digital database of soil and environmental data[33]

## 10. CLIMATE / WEATHER

### **ERA5 Reanalysis (ECMWF)**
- **Source:** European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts / Copernicus Climate Change Service
- **Resolution/Scale:** 0.25° x 0.25° (~31km)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1940-present (hourly data)
- **Update Frequency:** Daily (5-day delay)
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - CDS API
- **URL:** https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels
- **Quality Assessment:** Best global reanalysis dataset for Iraq. Hourly estimates of temperature, precipitation, wind, pressure, humidity. 137 vertical levels. Used extensively in Iraq climate studies[34][35][36][37][38]

### **World Bank Climate Change Knowledge Portal - Iraq**
- **Source:** World Bank (uses ERA5 data)
- **Resolution/Scale:** 25km (ERA5-based)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1950-2023 historical; projections to 2100
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - data download available
- **URL:** https://climateknowledgeportal.worldbank.org/country/iraq/
- **Quality Assessment:** User-friendly interface for Iraq-specific climate data. Pre-processed ERA5 data with climate indicators. Excellent for baseline climatology[39][34]

### **WorldClim v2.1**
- **Resolution/Scale:** 30 arc-seconds (~1km) to 10 arc-minutes
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1970-2000 (historical), future projections available
- **Update Frequency:** Major updates every few years
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** No (direct download)
- **URL:** https://www.worldclim.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** High-resolution interpolated climate surfaces (temperature, precipitation, bioclimatic variables). Widely used for spatial modeling[40]

### **Iraq Meteorological Organization and Seismology (IMOS)**
- **Source:** Iraqi government
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Request
- **API:** No
- **Quality Assessment:** National meteorological service operates weather stations across Iraq. Historical and real-time data available but access requires official request. Most authoritative for ground station data[38][40]

## 11. ADMINISTRATIVE BOUNDARIES

### **Iraq Common Operational Dataset - Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB)**
- **Source:** Iraq Central Statistics Office / OCHA / HDX
- **Resolution/Scale:** Levels 0-3 (Country, Governorate, District, Sub-district)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (reviewed December 2024)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual review
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - ArcGIS REST Services (geoservices)
- **URL:** https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-irq
- **Quality Assessment:** HIGHEST QUALITY for Iraq. Official boundaries from Iraq Central Statistics Office. 18 governorates (admin 1), 101 districts (admin 2), 294 sub-districts (admin 3). Geoservices available. Most authoritative administrative boundaries[41][42]

**Geoservices:**
- https://codgis.itos.uga.edu/arcgis/rest/services/COD_External/IRQ_EN (MapServer)
- https://codgis.itos.uga.edu/arcgis/rest/services/COD_External/IRQ_pcode (FeatureServer)

### **GADM (Database of Global Administrative Areas)**
- **Source:** GADM project
- **Resolution/Scale:** Levels 0-3
- **Temporal Coverage:** Version 4.1 (updated 2022)
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular updates
- **Access Type:** Free/Open (non-commercial)
- **API:** No (direct download)
- **URL:** https://gadm.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Good alternative but HDX COD-AB is more current and official for Iraq. GADM widely used in research[43][44][45][46]

## 12. POPULATION / DEMOGRAPHICS

### **WorldPop Iraq**
- **Source:** WorldPop / University of Southampton
- **Resolution/Scale:** 100m (~3 arc-seconds)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000-2020 (annual)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual updates
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **API:** Yes - via FTP and Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/listing?id=29 (Iraq-specific)
- **Quality Assessment:** HIGHEST QUALITY population dataset for Iraq. Provides population counts and density adjusted to UNPD estimates. UN-backed. Both top-down (census-based) and bottom-up (building/settlement) approaches[47][48][49][50][51]

### **LandScan**
- **Source:** Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL)
- **Resolution/Scale:** 1km (~30 arc-seconds)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Annual (2000-present)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access Type:** Free for research (registration required)
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://landscan.ornl.gov/
- **Quality Assessment:** Ambient population (24-hour average). Different methodology than WorldPop. Good for infrastructure planning. Iraq coverage available[51]

### **Iraq Central Statistics Office Census Data**
- **Source:** Iraq Central Statistics Office
- **Access Type:** Some data free, detailed data restricted
- **URL:** Contact COSIT directly
- **Quality Assessment:** Most authoritative but spatial data access limited. Latest census data from 2018 MICS (Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey)[41][43]

## 14. SPECIAL DATASETS FOR IRAQ

### **Archaeological Sites**

**QADIS Survey Archaeological Database**
- **Source:** University of Bologna / State Board of Antiquities and Heritage (SBAH)
- **Resolution/Scale:** Site-level (558 sites in southern Iraq)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2016-2019 survey
- **Update Frequency:** Static (project completed)
- **Access Type:** Academic/Research request
- **API:** No
- **Quality Assessment:** Most comprehensive recent archaeological risk assessment for southern Iraq. Remote sensing + ground control. Documents site locations, damage, threats. Critical for pipeline routing to avoid cultural heritage[58][59]

**UNESCO World Heritage Sites**
- **Source:** UNESCO / UNOSAT
- **Coverage:** Multiple sites including Mesopotamian Marshes, Ashur, Hatra, Erbil Citadel, Samarra
- **Access Type:** Free/Open
- **URL:** https://whc.unesco.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Official boundaries for World Heritage Sites. Damage assessments available[60][21]

### **Geological Hazards**

**Iraq Geological Hazards Classification Map**
- **Source:** Iraqi geoscientists (Sissakian et al. 2011)
- **Coverage:** 15 types of geological hazards documented and zoned
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2011
- **Access Type:** Academic literature
- **Quality Assessment:** Comprehensive hazard classification including landslides, sinkholes, subsidence, earthquakes, flooding. Maps zones by time and intensity[61]

**World Bank Iraq Seismic Hazard Assessment**
- **Source:** World Bank
- **Coverage:** Probabilistic Seismic Hazard Assessment (PSHA) maps for Iraq
- **Access Type:** World Bank documents (free)
- **URL:** https://documents.worldbank.org/
- **Quality Assessment:** Includes seismic hazard zones and landslide hazard maps for Iraq. Important for pipeline routing in seismically active areas (Kurdistan, Zagros Mountains)[62]

### **Conflict/Security Data**

**ACLED (Armed Conflict Location & Event Data Project)**
- **Source:** ACLED
- **Resolution/Scale:** Event locations
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1997-present (updated weekly)
- **Update Frequency:** Weekly
- **Access Type:** Free (registration required) / Commercial for bulk access
- **API:** Yes - ACLED API
- **URL:** https://acleddata.com/
- **Quality Assessment:** Comprehensive conflict event database. Critical for security assessment along pipeline routes. Shows incident locations, types, dates[44][45]

### **Military Zones / Restricted Areas**
- **Access Type:** Not publicly available
- **Quality Assessment:** Official military zone boundaries not published for security reasons. Local consultation required

### **Tribal/Indigenous Lands**
- **Access Type:** Limited documentation
- **Quality Assessment:** Traditional tribal boundaries not formally mapped in open GIS databases. Local stakeholder engagement essential

## KEY RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IRAQ PIPELINE ROUTING:

**CRITICAL DATA GAPS:**
1. **Oil & gas infrastructure:** Iraq has one of the lowest data quality scores globally (<0.3). National oil company data is confidential. Commercial providers (Bayphase, IHS Markit) offer best data but at high cost.[1]

2. **Road/rail networks:** Official government datasets not publicly available. Rely on OpenStreetMap with quality verification.

3. **Utilities:** Power transmission and telecom infrastructure poorly documented in open data.

4. **Hydrology:** Ministry of Water Resources data requires formal request.

**BEST OPEN DATA STRATEGY:**
- **Elevation:** Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (best)
- **Land cover:** ESA WorldCover 10m
- **Imagery:** Sentinel-2 (free baseline), commercial for detailed analysis
- **Boundaries:** HDX COD-AB (official)
- **Population:** WorldPop 100m
- **Climate:** ERA5 reanalysis
- **Soil:** FAO HWSD v2.0
- **Protected areas:** WDPA + contact Nature Iraq for KBA data

**PRIORITY ACTIONS:**
1. Budget for commercial datasets (pipeline infrastructure, high-res imagery, road networks)
2. Establish formal data sharing agreements with Iraqi government ministries
3. Conduct field surveys to verify OpenStreetMap data quality in project area
4. Engage local stakeholders for tribal lands, archaeological sites, security information
5. Use archaeological site buffers from QADIS survey and UNESCO data
6. Account for seismic/landslide hazards in northern Iraq (Zagros Mountains region)

Iraq's data landscape is challenging but workable with combination of global open datasets, commercial data investment, and local partnerships.[2][1]
