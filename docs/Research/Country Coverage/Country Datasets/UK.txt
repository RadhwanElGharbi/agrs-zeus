# UK GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### National LiDAR Programme (England)
- **Source/Provider**: Environment Agency
- **Resolution/Scale**: 1m and 2m spatial resolution
- **Temporal Coverage**: 1998-2019 (ongoing updates), ~99% coverage of England
- **Update Frequency**: Ongoing surveys, composite updated periodically
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: No dedicated API, download via data.gov.uk
- **Official URL**: https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/f0db0249-f17b-4036-9e65-309148c97ce4/national-lidar-programme
- **Quality Assessment**: Excellent - highest resolution national DEM available for England, centimeter-range vertical accuracy from LiDAR, comprehensive coverage[1][2][3]

### OS Terrain 5
- **Source/Provider**: Ordnance Survey
- **Resolution/Scale**: 5m grid spacing
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current (regularly updated)
- **Update Frequency**: Regular updates
- **Access Type**: Commercial/Licensed (part of OS premium products)
- **API Availability**: Yes, via OS Data Hub APIs
- **Official URL**: https://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/products/os-terrain-5
- **Quality Assessment**: Very high quality, national coverage of Great Britain, derived from multiple sources including LiDAR and photogrammetry[4]

### OS Terrain 50
- **Source/Provider**: Ordnance Survey
- **Resolution/Scale**: 50m grid spacing
- **Temporal Coverage**: Current
- **Update Frequency**: Periodic updates
- **Access Type**: Open/Free (part of OS OpenData)
- **API Availability**: Yes, via OS Data Hub
- **Official URL**: https://osdatahub.os.uk/downloads/open
- **Quality Assessment**: Good for national-scale analysis, less suitable for detailed routing, complete Great Britain coverage[5][6]

### Copernicus DEM GLO-30
- **Source/Provider**: European Space Agency (Copernicus Programme)
- **Resolution/Scale**: 30m (GLO-30) and 90m (GLO-90)
- **Temporal Coverage**: Based on data acquired 2011-2015
- **Update Frequency**: Static (2019 and 2020 releases)
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes, via Sentinel Hub and AWS
- **Official URL**: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/ and https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/
- **Quality Assessment**: Good global coverage including UK, suitable for regional analysis but lower resolution than national LiDAR products[7][8][9]

## 2. LAND COVER / LAND USE

### UKCEH Land Cover Maps (LCM2021)
- **Source/Provider**: UK Centre for Ecology & Hydrology (UKCEH)
- **Resolution/Scale**: 25m raster, 0.5ha minimum mappable unit for vector
- **Temporal Coverage**: Annual maps from 1990, 2007, 2015, 2017-2021
- **Update Frequency**: Annual since 2017
- **Access Type**: Licensed/Commercial (various pricing tiers)
- **API Availability**: Yes, WMS services available
- **Official URL**: https://www.ceh.ac.uk/data/ukceh-land-cover-maps
- **Quality Assessment**: Excellent - most authoritative national land cover, based on UK BAP Broad Habitats, high accuracy validated dataset[10][11][12]

### ESA WorldCover 2020/2021
- **Source/Provider**: European Space Agency
- **Resolution/Scale**: 10m resolution
- **Temporal Coverage**: 2020 and 2021 baseline products
- **Update Frequency**: Annual updates planned
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes, via Copernicus Data Space
- **Official URL**: https://worldcover2020.esa.int
- **Quality Assessment**: Very good - 75% overall accuracy, 11 land cover classes, global coverage with excellent UK detail[13][14]

### ESRI 2020 Global LULC from Sentinel-2
- **Source/Provider**: ESRI (using Sentinel-2 data)
- **Resolution/Scale**: 10m resolution
- **Temporal Coverage**: 2020
- **Update Frequency**: Periodic updates
- **Access Type**: Open/Free
- **API Availability**: Yes, via ArcGIS Living Atlas
- **Official URL**: https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/landcoverexplorer/
- **Quality Assessment**: Very good - 10 classes, deep learning-based classification, complements WorldCover[15][16]

- **Temporal Coverage**: Current and forecast data

- **Access Type**: Subscription-based (various tiers)

- **Official URL**: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu
- **Quality Assessment**: Excellent for long-term climate analysis, historical weather patterns, complements Met Office data[web:general knowledge]

### WorldClim
- **Source/Provider**: WorldClim project

## Key Recommendations for UK Pipeline Routing:

**Priority National Datasets:**
1. Environment Agency 1m LiDAR for England - highest quality terrain data[2][1]
2. OS Terrain 5 and OS NGD Transport Network for detailed infrastructure[4][23]
3. UKCEH Land Cover Maps for current land use[12][10]
4. BGS Geology 50K and GeoSure for geological constraints[45]
5. Natural England and JNCC protected areas for environmental constraints[41][40][43]
6. Historic England datasets for archaeological constraints[62][63]

**API Access Strengths:**
- OS Data Hub provides comprehensive API access to premium UK mapping[5][23]
- Met Office DataPoint and Weather DataHub for weather/climate API[51][52][53]
- NESO Data Portal for energy infrastructure[32][31]
- Copernicus Data Space for free satellite imagery and DEM[8][59]

**Coverage Notes:**
- Scotland: Some datasets cover only England/Wales - verify Scottish coverage separately
- Northern Ireland: Often requires separate datasets from Northern Ireland agencies
- Offshore: NSTA provides excellent offshore oil/gas infrastructure data[35][36]
