# Comprehensive GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing in Canada

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### National Geological Survey DEMs

**Canadian Digital Elevation Model (CDEM)**
- **Source:** Natural Resources Canada (NRCan)
- **Resolution:** 0.75 arc-seconds (~23 meters), varying by latitude
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1945-2011
- **Update Frequency:** Legacy product, superseded by HRDEM
- **Access:** Open/Free (Open Government Licence - Canada)
- **API:** Yes, Google Earth Engine API available
- **URL:** https://open.canada.ca & https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/NRCan_CDEM
- **Quality:** Moderate resolution; elevations represent either ground or reflective surface; suitable for regional planning but less accurate than newer products[1][2]

**High Resolution Digital Elevation Model (HRDEM)**
- **Source:** Natural Resources Canada
- **Resolution:** 1-2 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2006-present (ongoing)
- **Update Frequency:** Ongoing coverage expansion under National Elevation Data Strategy
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Available through Data Cube
- **URL:** https://natural-resources.canada.ca/science-data/data-analysis/geospatial-data-tools-services/elevation-data
- **Quality:** Excellent; derived from LiDAR and high-resolution satellite imagery; includes Digital Surface Model (DSM) and Digital Terrain Model (DTM); accuracy of 1-2m for satellite-derived, centimeters for LiDAR[3][1]

**Medium Resolution Digital Elevation Model (MRDEM)**
- **Source:** Natural Resources Canada
- **Resolution:** 30 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** Integrates Copernicus (2011-2015) with LiDAR (2006-2023)
- **Update Frequency:** Updated as new LiDAR becomes available
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Available through Data Cube
- **URL:** https://natural-resources.canada.ca/maps-tools-publications/satellite-elevation-air-photos/national-elevation-data-strategy
- **Quality:** Very good; complete Canada coverage; referenced to CGVD2013; includes DSM and DTM[3]

### LiDAR Point Clouds

**Airborne LiDAR Point Clouds**
- **Source:** Natural Resources Canada
- **Resolution:** Sub-meter (centimeter accuracy)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Various acquisition projects 2006-present
- **Update Frequency:** Ongoing acquisitions
- **Access:** Open/Free (Cloud Optimized Point Cloud format)
- **API:** Direct file access via URLs
- **URL:** Coverage map at https://natural-resources.canada.ca/science-data/data-analysis/geospatial-data-tools-services/elevation-data
- **Quality:** Excellent; highest accuracy available; COPC format compatible with QGIS 3.26+; captures ground and vegetation[3]

### Best Global DEM for Canada

**Copernicus DEM**
- **Source:** European Space Agency (integrated in MRDEM)
- **Resolution:** 30 meters (integrated version)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2011-2015
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes, via Copernicus services
- **URL:** Integrated in NRCan MRDEM products
- **Quality:** Good global baseline; less accurate than national products[3]

## 2. LAND COVER / LAND USE

### National Land Use Maps

**Land Cover Register (LCR)**
- **Source:** Statistics Canada (Census of Environment program)
- **Resolution:** 30 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** Circa 2020
- **Update Frequency:** Occasional basis (not annual)
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Not specified
- **URL:** https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/16-510-x/16-510-x2025003-eng.htm
- **Quality:** Very good; 11-class classification system; integrates multiple datasets (AAFC SDLU, CCMEO LCC, CFS LCV2); designed for ecosystem accounting and spatial analysis[4]

**AAFC Annual Crop Inventory**
- **Source:** Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada (AAFC)
- **Resolution:** 30 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** Annual updates (2013-present)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes, available through Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://open.canada.ca (search "AAFC Annual Crop Inventory")
- **Quality:** Excellent for agricultural areas; covers south of 60°N latitude; detailed crop classification[5][4]

### Best Global Datasets

**ESA WorldCover**
- **Source:** European Space Agency
- **Resolution:** 10 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2020-2021
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes
- **URL:** https://esa-worldcover.org
- **Quality:** Excellent; 11 land cover classes; high resolution suitable for detailed planning[6]

**Sentinel-2 Land Cover**
- **Source:** ESA Copernicus program
- **Resolution:** 10-20 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous/5-day revisit
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes, multiple APIs including Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://scihub.copernicus.eu
- **Quality:** Excellent temporal resolution; suitable for change detection[7]

## 3. HYDROLOGY / WATER

### National Hydrography Networks

**National Hydro Network (NHN)**
- **Source:** Natural Resources Canada (GeoBase program)
- **Resolution:** 1:50,000 (some at 1:10,000-1:20,000)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current, continuously updated
- **Update Frequency:** At least annually
- **Access:** Open/Free (Open Government Licence)
- **API:** Available through WMS/WFS services
- **URL:** https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/a4b190fe-e090-4e6d-881e-b87956c07977
- **Quality:** Excellent; seamless vector dataset; includes lakes, reservoirs, rivers, streams, canals, drainage networks; attributes for flow analysis; uses Linear Referencing System; topologically structured[8][9][10]

**NHN Features Include:**
- Lakes and reservoirs
- Watercourses (rivers and streams)
- Canals
- Islands
- Obstacles (waterfalls, rapids, rocks)
- Constructions (dams, wharves, dikes)
- Linear drainage network
- Toponymic information (geographical names)[9]

### Best Global Datasets

**Global Surface Water Explorer**
- **Source:** European Commission Joint Research Centre
- **Resolution:** 30 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1984-present
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic updates
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes, Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://global-surface-water.appspot.com
- **Quality:** Good for temporal water dynamics; complements NHN for historical analysis[9]

## 4. INFRASTRUCTURE - ROADS

### National Road Network

**National Road Network (NRN)**
- **Source:** Statistics Canada (formerly GeoBase)
- **Resolution:** 1:10,000
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (March 2010 baseline, continuously updated)
- **Update Frequency:** At least annually
- **Access:** Open/Free (Open Government Licence)
- **API:** WMS available
- **URL:** https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/3d282116-e556-400c-9306-ca1a3cada77f
- **Quality:** Excellent; over 1 million km of road data; includes road segments, street names, types, directions, address ranges; provincial/territorial datasets; maintained through intergovernmental partnerships[11][12]

**Road Network File (RNF)**
- **Source:** Statistics Canada
- **Resolution:** Census-compatible road network
- **Temporal Coverage:** Updated with each census (2021 latest)
- **Update Frequency:** Census cycles (typically every 5 years)
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Limited
- **URL:** https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/geo/sip-pis/rnf-frr/index-eng.cfm
- **Quality:** Good; designed for statistical purposes; relative positional accuracy prioritized; not suitable for engineering/surveying applications[13][14]

### Commercial Providers

**HERE Technologies & TomTom**
- **Coverage:** Excellent for Canada
- **Resolution:** High detail, navigation-grade
- **Access:** Commercial/Purchase
- **API:** Yes, comprehensive APIs
- **Quality:** Very high; includes real-time traffic, routing attributes, one-way streets[12]

### OpenStreetMap

**OSM Canada Coverage**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap Community
- **Resolution:** Variable, generally good in urban areas
- **Access:** Open/Free (ODbL License)
- **API:** Yes, Overpass API
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org
- **Quality:** Good to excellent in major cities; variable in remote areas; strong volunteer community; continuously updated[12]

## 5. INFRASTRUCTURE - RAILWAYS

### National Railway Network

**National Railway Network (NRWN)**
- **Source:** Natural Resources Canada (GeoBase Series)
- **Resolution:** High-quality geometric and attributive data
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic updates
- **Access:** Open/Free (Open Government Licence)
- **API:** WMS/WFS available
- **URL:** https://open.canada.ca (search "National Railway Network NRWN")
- **Quality:** Very good; geometric and attributive description of Canadian rail network; includes freight and passenger rail[15][16]

**Provincial Railway Networks**
- **Ontario Railway Network (ORWN)**
- **Source:** Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources
- **Resolution:** Enhanced spatial and attribute data
- **Access:** Open via Ontario GeoHub
- **URL:** https://geohub.lio.gov.on.ca
- **Quality:** Excellent for Ontario; seven data classes[17]

### Commercial/Industry Sources

**Railway Association of Canada Rail Atlas**
- **Source:** Railway Association of Canada
- **Resolution:** Comprehensive network map
- **Temporal Coverage:** December 2023
- **Access:** Commercial/Purchase
- **URL:** https://www.railcan.ca/resources/rail-atlas/
- **Quality:** Excellent; shows all railway lines by track owner; includes ~300 rail facilities and 4,500 stations; covers Canada, US, and Mexico principal lines[18]

### OpenStreetMap Railways

**Coverage:** Good in major corridors; variable in remote areas
**Access:** Open/Free
**Quality:** Generally good but requires validation against official sources[16]

## 6. INFRASTRUCTURE - UTILITIES

### National Electricity Networks

**No centralized national electricity transmission GIS dataset publicly available**
- Provincial utilities maintain their own datasets (varies by province)
- **Access:** Typically restricted or requires formal request
- **Quality:** Provincial data quality is generally high but fragmented[19]

### OpenInfraMap / OpenStreetMap Utilities

**Source:** OpenStreetMap Community
**Coverage:** Partial; better in southern Canada
**Access:** Open/Free
**API:** Yes, Overpass API
**URL:** https://openinframap.org
**Quality:** Fair to good; incomplete coverage; useful for reference but requires field verification[12]

## 7. INFRASTRUCTURE - PIPELINES

### Federal Pipeline Data

**Canada Energy Regulator (CER) Pipeline Data**
- **Source:** Canada Energy Regulator (formerly National Energy Board)
- **Resolution:** High accuracy for federally regulated pipelines
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current, historical incident data from 2008
- **Update Frequency:** Continuously updated
- **Access:** Open/Free (interactive map); detailed data may require request
- **API:** ArcGIS REST API available
- **URL:** https://www.cer-rec.gc.ca/en/safety-environment/industry-performance/interactive-pipeline/
- **Quality:** Excellent for federally regulated pipelines (26 major systems); includes pipeline profiles, incident data, locations; does NOT include provincially regulated pipelines[20][21][22]

**Pipeline Profiles**
- 26 major pipeline systems with detailed data
- Interactive maps with story points
- Includes throughput, capacity, safety, environmental data
- Updated regularly[21]

### Provincial Pipeline Registries

**Provincial/territorial pipeline data varies significantly**
- British Columbia Energy Regulator maintains detailed spatial data
- Alberta Energy Regulator has comprehensive pipeline databases
- **Access:** Typically requires formal request or subscription
- **Quality:** Generally excellent within jurisdictions[23]

### OpenStreetMap Pipelines

**Coverage:** Very incomplete
**Quality:** Poor; not reliable for planning purposes; use only as supplementary reference[20]

## 8. PROTECTED AREAS / ENVIRONMENTAL

### National Protected Areas Database

**Canadian Protected and Conserved Areas Database (CPCAD)**
- **Source:** Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC)
- **Resolution:** Vector polygons at original survey scales
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current, historical boundaries
- **Update Frequency:** Regular updates (contributors added in 2023-2024)
- **Access:** Open/Free (Open Government Licence)
- **API:** WMS/WFS available
- **URL:** https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/national-wildlife-areas/protected-conserved-areas-database.html
- **Quality:** Excellent; most comprehensive database; includes federal, provincial, territorial, Indigenous, NGO, and private protected areas; marine and terrestrial; includes protected areas (PA) and other effective area-based conservation measures (OECM); endorsed by Pathway to Canada Target 1[24][25]

**Features:**
- National parks
- National wildlife areas (54 sites)
- National marine conservation areas
- Migratory bird sanctuaries
- Provincial/territorial protected areas
- Indigenous protected areas
- Conservation lands
- Area type designations (IUCN categories)[26][24]

### World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA)

**Source:** UNEP-WCMC
**Coverage:** Global, includes Canada
**Access:** Open/Free
**API:** Yes
**URL:** https://www.protectedplanet.net
**Quality:** Good for international context; CPCAD is more complete for Canada specifically[24]

## 9. GEOLOGY / SOIL

### National Geological Survey

**Canada Geological Map Compilation (CGMC)**
- **Source:** Geological Survey of Canada (Natural Resources Canada)
- **Resolution:** 1:30,000 to 1:2,000,000 (varies by source)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current compilation of historical surveys
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic compilation updates
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** WMS/WFS available
- **URL:** https://natural-resources.canada.ca/maps-tools-publications/maps/earth-sciences-maps
- **Quality:** Excellent; standardized compilation of 32 provincial/territorial geodatabases; includes lithology, mineralogy, metamorphic, lithostratigraphic data; hierarchical classification with 35 generalized lithology classes; linked to Lexicon of Canadian Geological Names (Weblex)[27][28]

**Surficial Geology Maps**
- **Source:** Geological Survey of Canada
- **Resolution:** Various scales
- **Coverage:** Comprehensive but variable by region
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **Quality:** Very good; standardized data model version 2.3.0; essential for understanding permafrost, landslides, and unstable terrain[29]

### Soil Maps

**National Soil Database (NSDB)**
- **Source:** Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada (AAFC)
- **Resolution:** Multiple scales (1:20,000 to 1:250,000)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical surveys through present
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic updates
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** WMS available
- **URL:** https://sis.agr.gc.ca/cansis/nsdb/index.html
- **Quality:** Very good; comprehensive soil, landscape, and climate data; includes Detailed Soil Surveys (DSS) for agricultural areas and Canada Land Inventory (CLI)[30]

**Soil Landscape Grids of Canada**
- **Source:** Canadian Soil Information Service (AAFC)
- **Resolution:** 100 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (trained on 10,000+ pedon locations)
- **Update Frequency:** Updated as new data available
- **Access:** Open/Free (under evaluation)
- **API:** Likely available
- **URL:** https://sis.agr.gc.ca/cansis/nsdb/psm/index.html
- **Quality:** Excellent; machine learning-derived; seven key soil properties at five depth increments; includes depth to bedrock; uses random forest model with 70 covariates; complete Canada coverage[31]

### Global Soil Datasets

**FAO HWSD / SoilGrids**
- **Source:** ISRIC - World Soil Information
- **Resolution:** 250 meters (SoilGrids250m 2.0)
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes, WCS and REST
- **URL:** https://soilgrids.org
- **Quality:** Good global baseline; Canadian national products are superior[32][33]

## 10. CLIMATE / WEATHER

### National Meteorological Service

**Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) Climate Data**
- **Source:** Meteorological Service of Canada
- **Resolution:** Station-based and gridded products (various)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical (1840s-present), current, forecasts
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time for current weather; historical updated regularly
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes, MSC GeoMet (WMS, WFS, OGC API)
- **URL:** https://climate.weather.gc.ca & https://api.weather.gc.ca
- **Quality:** Excellent; comprehensive historical and real-time data; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity, degree days; station network across Canada; MSC Datamart for raw data access[34][35][36][37]

**MSC GeoMet Platform:**
- GeoMet-Weather (real-time meteorological data)
- GeoMet-Climate (historical climate data)
- GeoMet-OGC-API (emerging OGC standards)
- Anonymous and free access
- Supports WMS, WCS, WFS protocols[37]

**Climate Data Extraction Tool**
- **URL:** https://climate-change.canada.ca/climate-data/
- Interactive tool for downloading specific datasets
- Includes climate normals, monthly summaries, daily data
- Future climate projections available[38]

### Global Climate Datasets

**ERA5 Reanalysis**
- **Source:** ECMWF/Copernicus
- **Resolution:** ~31 km (0.25 degrees)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1940-present
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes, CDS API
- **Quality:** Excellent; hourly data; best global reanalysis product[37]

**WorldClim**
- **Source:** WorldClim.org
- **Resolution:** ~1 km at highest resolution
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical averages
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **Quality:** Good for climatological averages; useful for baseline climate characterization[36]

## 11. ADMINISTRATIVE BOUNDARIES

### Official National Boundaries

**Statistics Canada Census Boundary Files**
- **Source:** Statistics Canada
- **Resolution:** Multiple levels (national to dissemination area)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Census 2021 (updated every 5 years)
- **Update Frequency:** Census cycles; adjusted to CanVec as of 2020
- **Access:** Open/Free (Open Government Licence)
- **API:** WMS/WFS available
- **URL:** https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/geo/sip-pis/boundary-limites/index2021-eng.cfm
- **Quality:** Excellent; authoritative; includes provinces/territories, census divisions, census subdivisions, dissemination areas, federal electoral districts, health regions, economic regions; cartographic and digital versions available[39][40]

**Boundary Types:**
- Cartographic (major land mass only)
- Digital (full extent including coastal waters)
- Administrative areas (provinces, territories, federal districts)
- Statistical areas (population centres, economic regions, dissemination areas)
- Land and water area calculations included[39]

**CanVec Administrative Features**
- **Source:** Natural Resources Canada
- **Resolution:** High accuracy
- **Coverage:** Geopolitical regions, populated places
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **Quality:** Excellent; basis for Statistics Canada boundaries since 2020[41][40]

### Global Administrative Boundaries

**GADM (Database of Global Administrative Areas)**
- **Source:** GADM.org
- **Resolution:** Multiple administrative levels
- **Access:** Open/Free for non-commercial
- **API:** Direct download
- **URL:** https://gadm.org
- **Quality:** Good; Statistics Canada products are more authoritative for Canada[41]

**Statistics Canada Census of Population**
- **Source:** Statistics Canada
- **Resolution:** Multiple geographic levels (dissemination areas to provinces)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Census 2021 (quinquennial)
- **Update Frequency:** Every 5 years
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Web Data Service available
- **URL:** https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/index-eng.cfm
- **Quality:** Excellent; authoritative; includes age, income, education, families, housing, immigration, Indigenous peoples, labour force; spatially linked to boundary files[42][43]

**Census Data Categories:**
- Population density
- Age/sex structure
- Household composition
- Urban/rural classification
- Population centres
- Indigenous identity
- Income and employment[42]

### Global Population Datasets

**WorldPop**
- **Source:** WorldPop Research Group
- **Resolution:** 100 meters (1 km products also available)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Annual estimates, historical and projected
- **Access:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes, REST API (woprVision)

**LandScan Global**

### Landslide Hazard Maps

**Key Factors Mapped:**
- Slope stability in permafrost terrain

- Historical landslide inventory[55]

### Military Zones / Restricted Areas

**Department of National Defence**
- **Access:** Restricted/Requires formal request

## SUMMARY QUALITY RANKINGS BY CATEGORY

**Highest Priority/Quality Canadian Sources:**
1. **Elevation:** HRDEM (1-2m LiDAR/satellite) > MRDEM (30m) > CDEM (23m legacy)
2. **Land Cover:** Land Cover Register (30m) + AAFC Crop Inventory (30m annual)
3. **Hydrology:** National Hydro Network (1:50,000 seamless)
4. **Roads:** National Road Network (1:10,000)
5. **Railways:** National Railway Network (NRWN)
6. **Protected Areas:** CPCAD (most comprehensive)
7. **Geology:** Canada Geological Map Compilation
8. **Soil:** Soil Landscape Grids 100m + NSDB
9. **Climate:** ECCC MSC GeoMet with full API access
10. **Boundaries:** Statistics Canada Census Boundaries
11. **Pipelines:** CER Pipeline Data (federal only)
12. **Indigenous Lands:** ISC Map Room + ATRIS
13. **Permafrost:** PIN + GSC surficial geology
14. **Seismic:** Earthquakes Canada hazard maps

**Key Advantages for Canadian Pipeline Routing:**
- Most foundational datasets are open/free with permissive licensing
- Strong API availability (WMS/WFS) for integration
- National standardization through GeoBase and Statistics Canada
- Regular update cycles for dynamic datasets
- High resolution in critical infrastructure corridors
- Comprehensive northern/permafrost data for Arctic routing[1][8][30][24][37][20][12]
