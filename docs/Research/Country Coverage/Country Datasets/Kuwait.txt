# GIS Datasets for Oil & Gas Pipeline Routing in Kuwait

## 1. ELEVATION/TERRAIN (DEM)

### **Copernicus DEM GLO-30**
- **Source:** European Space Agency / Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution:** 30 meters (GLO-30 Public where available, 90m elsewhere)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (static dataset based on 2011-2015 data)
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Sentinel Hub API, Copernicus Data Space API
- **URL:** https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/ and https://portal.opentopography.org/
- **Quality:** Excellent - Most accurate global DEM for Kuwait, superior to SRTM with RMSE typically 2-6m vertically[1][2][3]

### **SRTM 30m DEM**
- **Source:** NASA/USGS
- **Resolution:** 30 meters (1 arc-second)
- **Temporal Coverage:** February 2000
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - USGS Earth Explorer API
- **URL:** https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov
- **Quality:** Good - RMSE ~6-10m vertically, widely validated, suitable for regional pipeline routing[4][5]

### **ASTER GDEM v3**
- **Source:** NASA/METI
- **Resolution:** 30 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000-2013
- **Update Frequency:** Static (version 3 released 2019)
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - NASA Earthdata API
- **URL:** https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov
- **Quality:** Moderate - Less accurate than SRTM and Copernicus in flat desert terrain, RMSE 8-15m[5]

### **Kuwait Survey Department Topographic Data**
- **Source:** Kuwait Survey Department, Ministry of Public Works
- **Resolution:** 1:10,000 orthophoto maps (688 sheets), 1:25,000 line mapping
- **Temporal Coverage:** Originally captured 1960s-1980s by Hunting Surveys and Asia Air Survey
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular updates
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Requires request from government
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact Survey Department directly
- **Quality:** High accuracy for historical baseline, may be outdated; integrated into KUDAMS system using Kuwait Transverse Mercator grid[6][7]

### **Kuwait Oil Company (KOC) LiDAR DEM**
- **Source:** Kuwait Oil Company
- **Resolution:** 10 cm accuracy from recent aerial surveys
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2010s-present (ongoing updates)
- **Update Frequency:** Project-based updates
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Internal KOC use, may require formal request
- **API:** No public API
- **URL:** Contact KOC directly
- **Quality:** Excellent - Highest resolution DEM available for Kuwait oil field areas, three-dimensional terrain mapping[8][9]

## 2. LAND COVER / LAND USE

### **ESA WorldCover**
- **Source:** European Space Agency WorldCover Consortium
- **Resolution:** 10 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2020, 2021 (annual products)
- **Update Frequency:** Annually
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Sentinel Hub API, Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://esa-worldcover.org/ and https://worldcover2021.esa.int/download
- **Quality:** Excellent - 11 land cover classes, combines Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical, ~75% overall accuracy, ideal for Kuwait's desert and coastal environments[10][11][12]

### **Google Dynamic World (Sentinel-2 based)**
- **Source:** Google/World Resources Institute
- **Resolution:** 10 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present
- **Update Frequency:** Near real-time (every Sentinel-2 pass)
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Google Earth Engine API
- **URL:** https://www.dynamicworld.app/
- **Quality:** Very good - Near real-time updates, 9 land cover classes, useful for monitoring construction and land use changes

### **Kuwait EPA eMISK Land Cover Data**
- **Source:** Kuwait Environment Public Authority (EPA)
- **Resolution:** Variable (integrated with topographic, geomorphological data)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current - 20+ years of monitoring data
- **Update Frequency:** Ongoing updates
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Government agency access via eMISK Enterprise system
- **API:** No public API
- **URL:** https://epa.gov.kw/en-us/eMISK and www.beatona.net
- **Quality:** High - Customized for Kuwait environmental management, integrates 11 environmental domains with 250+ geographic layers[13][14][15]

### **Sentinel-2 Land Cover Classification (Custom)**
- **Source:** ESA Copernicus Programme
- **Resolution:** 10-20 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015-present
- **Update Frequency:** 5-day revisit time
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Copernicus Open Access Hub API, Google Earth Engine
- **URL:** https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/
- **Quality:** Excellent - Raw imagery for custom classification, 13 spectral bands, suitable for desert vegetation mapping

## 3. HYDROLOGY / WATER

### **Kuwait EPA Water Resources Database**
- **Source:** Kuwait Environment Public Authority
- **Resolution:** Variable - includes wells, aquifers, wadis
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current - 20+ years monitoring
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous monitoring
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Government access via eMISK
- **API:** No public API
- **URL:** https://epa.gov.kw/en-us/eMISK
- **Quality:** Excellent - Most authoritative source for Kuwait water resources, includes fresh and brackish groundwater, wadis, drainage networks[7][15]

### **Kuwait Institute for Scientific Research (KISR) Hydrological Data**
- **Source:** Kuwait Institute for Scientific Research
- **Resolution:** Variable - includes bathymetric and hydrological mapping
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1980s-present
- **Update Frequency:** Project-based
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Requires request
- **API:** No
- **URL:** www.kisr.edu.kw (contact directly)
- **Quality:** High - Environmental GIS with detailed hydrological and geomorphological data for Kuwait[6]

### **Global Surface Water Explorer**
- **Source:** European Commission Joint Research Centre
- **Resolution:** 30 meters
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1984-present
- **Update Frequency:** Annual updates
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Google Earth Engine API
- **URL:** https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/
- **Quality:** Very good - Limited natural surface water in Kuwait, but excellent for coastal areas and seasonal water bodies

### **HydroSHEDS / HydroRIVERS**
- **Source:** WWF/McGill University/USGS
- **Resolution:** 15 arc-seconds (~500m)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (static baseline)
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic major updates
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** No - Download only
- **URL:** https://www.hydrosheds.org/
- **Quality:** Good for global coverage, but limited utility for Kuwait due to lack of perennial rivers; useful for wadi mapping and catchment delineation[16][17][18]

### **GLWD v2 (Global Lakes and Wetlands Database)**
- **Source:** McGill University
- **Resolution:** 15 arc-seconds (~500m)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1990-2020
- **Update Frequency:** Static
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/glwd
- **Quality:** Moderate - Limited wetlands in Kuwait, primarily relevant for coastal areas[19]

## 4. INFRASTRUCTURE - ROADS

### **Kuwait Survey Department Road Network**
- **Source:** Kuwait Survey Department / Public Authority for Civil Information (PACI)
- **Resolution:** High precision - street level with building footprints
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current - continuously updated via Kuwait Finder system
- **Update Frequency:** Regular updates using deep learning/AI on satellite imagery
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Government use, may require formal request
- **API:** Likely internal only
- **URL:** Contact PACI or Survey Department
- **Quality:** Excellent - Most authoritative and current road network for Kuwait, includes ring roads and detailed urban street networks[20][6]

### **HERE Technologies Kuwait Roads**
- **Source:** HERE Technologies
- **Resolution:** Road centerline with attributes (speed limits, road class, restrictions)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Quarterly updates
- **Access Type:** Commercial/Purchase
- **API:** Yes - HERE Location Services API
- **URL:** https://www.here.com/
- **Quality:** Excellent - High-quality commercial dataset with detailed road attributes, routing restrictions, widely used in oil & gas industry

### **TomTom Kuwait Roads**
- **Source:** TomTom
- **Resolution:** Road centerline with traffic data
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Regular updates
- **Access Type:** Commercial/Purchase
- **API:** Yes - TomTom Maps API, Routing API
- **URL:** https://developer.tomtom.com/
- **Quality:** Excellent - Includes real-time traffic data, 24% congestion level in Kuwait City, comprehensive road network coverage[21][22][23]

### **OpenStreetMap Kuwait**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap Foundation / Community contributors
- **Resolution:** Variable - road centerlines
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current - continuously updated
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous community updates
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API, OSM API
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/
- **Quality:** Good - ~31,100 km of roads mapped (~48% of total estimated), includes ring road classifications (1st-7th Ring Roads), major routes like Gulf Street (Route 25), Jahra Road (Route 80)[24][25]

## 5. INFRASTRUCTURE - RAILWAYS

### **Kuwait National Railway Project (KNRR) Data**
- **Source:** Public Authority for Roads and Land Transport (PART) / Ministry of Communications
- **Resolution:** Engineering design level
- **Temporal Coverage:** In development - planned completion 2028
- **Update Frequency:** Project development phase
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Government/contractor access
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.kuwaitrailroad.com/ and www.moc.kw
- **Quality:** Not yet operational - 265km double-track network planned (111km GCC component to Saudi border, freight line to Boubyan Port), 120-200 km/h speeds[26][27][28]

### **Gulf Railway Network (GCC Railway)**
- **Source:** GCC Railway Authority
- **Resolution:** Regional network planning
- **Temporal Coverage:** Under development - scheduled 2030
- **Update Frequency:** Project phase
- **Access Type:** Restricted
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact GCC Railway Authority
- **Quality:** Future infrastructure - 2,177 km network connecting six Gulf countries, Kuwait segment in design phase[29][30]

### **OpenStreetMap Kuwait Railways**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap Community
- **Resolution:** Vector line data
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Community updates
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/
- **Quality:** Limited - Minimal existing railway infrastructure in Kuwait, primarily future planned routes

## 6. INFRASTRUCTURE - UTILITIES (Power, Telecom)

### **Kuwait Ministry of Electricity and Water (MEW) GIS Data**
- **Source:** Ministry of Electricity and Water
- **Resolution:** High precision - facility and network level
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical archive converted from paper maps + current digital data
- **Update Frequency:** Continuous updates
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Government/utility use
- **API:** Likely internal only
- **URL:** https://www.mew.gov.kw/
- **Quality:** Excellent - Comprehensive electricity transmission and water distribution networks, 16,000+ digitized maps, ArcFM-based utility GIS covering 3+ million consumers[31][32]

### **OpenStreetMap / OpenInfraMap Kuwait Utilities**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap Community
- **Resolution:** Variable
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Community updates
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API
- **URL:** https://openinframap.org/ and https://www.openstreetmap.org/
- **Quality:** Limited - Incomplete coverage for Kuwait, useful for public infrastructure mapping but not comprehensive for pipeline routing

## 7. INFRASTRUCTURE - PIPELINES

### **Kuwait Oil Company (KOC) Pipeline GIS Database**
- **Source:** Kuwait Oil Company - Integrated Masterplan (IMP) Project
- **Resolution:** Asset-level precision - includes all wells, pipelines, manifolds
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current - continuously updated
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time updates via field surveys and aerial imagery
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Internal KOC use only, extremely sensitive data
- **API:** Internal ArcGIS Server/Portal APIs only
- **URL:** Contact KOC directly (formal request required)
- **Quality:** Excellent - Most comprehensive and accurate pipeline database for Kuwait, includes aboveground/underground pipelines, pipe diameters, crude types, metal tag IDs, built using ArcSDE geodatabase with CAD integration[33][34][35][36][8]

### **Kuwait National Petroleum Company (KNPC) Infrastructure Data**
- **Source:** Kuwait National Petroleum Company
- **Resolution:** Facility and network level
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Regular updates
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Internal use, requires formal government request
- **API:** No public API
- **URL:** Contact KNPC directly
- **Quality:** High - Geological and infrastructure mapping at 1:50,000, 1:100,000, 1:250,000 scales prepared by Hunting Geology and Geophysics (1981)[6]

### **OpenStreetMap Kuwait Pipelines**
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap Community
- **Resolution:** Line features
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Community updates
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Overpass API
- **URL:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/
- **Quality:** Very limited - Incomplete and not authoritative, should not be relied upon for pipeline routing decisions

## 8. PROTECTED AREAS / ENVIRONMENTAL

### **Kuwait EPA Protected Areas Database**
- **Source:** Kuwait Environment Public Authority
- **Resolution:** Vector polygons with boundaries
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current - established under 2016 environment protection law
- **Update Frequency:** Updated as new areas designated
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Access via eMISK system or www.beatona.net
- **API:** No public API
- **URL:** https://epa.gov.kw/ and www.beatona.net
- **Quality:** Excellent - Most authoritative source for Kuwait protected areas, wildlife reserves, conservation areas[14][15]

### **World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA)**
- **Source:** UNEP-WCMC / IUCN
- **Resolution:** Vector polygons
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current - updated September 2025
- **Update Frequency:** Monthly updates
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Protected Planet API
- **URL:** https://www.protectedplanet.net/en/country/KWT
- **Quality:** Good - 39 protected areas reported (15 terrestrial covering 17.1% of Kuwait, 3 marine covering 1.4%), includes proposed areas; national reporting shows 9.3% terrestrial coverage[37][38][39][40]

### **Kuwait National Parks and Reserves**
- **Source:** Kuwait EPA / Ministry of Environment
- **Resolution:** Vector boundaries
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** As designated
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Requires request
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact Kuwait EPA
- **Quality:** High - Includes marine protected areas (3 implemented, 19 proposed covering 126 km²)[41]

## 9. GEOLOGY / SOIL

### **Kuwait Geological Survey Maps**
- **Source:** Geologische Bundesanstalt (GBA) Austria / Kuwait Oil Company
- **Resolution:** 1:250,000 (best available), 1:100,000, 1:50,000 scales
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1981 compilation (based on LANDSAT imagery)
- **Update Frequency:** Historic - not regularly updated
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Requires request
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact Kuwait government geological authorities
- **Quality:** Good - GBA 1:250,000 compilation remains best available geological mapping of Kuwait[6]

### **Kuwait Soil Survey (1999)**
- **Source:** Government of Kuwait / AACM International Consultant
- **Resolution:** 1:100,000 reconnaissance, 1:25,000 semi-detailed
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1999 (field survey)
- **Update Frequency:** No regular updates
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Requires request from agricultural authorities
- **API:** No
- **URL:** Contact Kuwait Agricultural Department or KISR
- **Quality:** Very good - 8,400 observation points covering 16,800 km², 105 soil profiles described, 570 horizons analyzed, includes soil taxa classification (Haplocalcids, Petrocalcids, Haplogypsids) following USDA-NRCS standards[42][43]

### **FAO Reconnaissance Soil Survey (1965-1968)**
- **Source:** FAO / Kuwait Ministry of Public Works
- **Resolution:** 1:250,000, published at 1:500,000
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1965-1968 field surveys
- **Update Frequency:** Historic dataset
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/
- **Quality:** Moderate - Historic baseline, includes soil irrigability classifications and rangeland suitability, outdated but useful for regional context[44]

### **FAO Harmonized World Soil Database (HWSD)**
- **Source:** FAO / IIASA
- **Resolution:** 1 km (30 arc-seconds)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current (compilation of various sources)
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic major updates
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** No - Download only
- **URL:** https://www.fao.org/soils-portal/
- **Quality:** Moderate - Global coverage, lower resolution than national surveys, suitable for regional assessment

### **OneGeology Portal**
- **Source:** OneGeology Consortium / USGS
- **Resolution:** Variable by contributing survey
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Contributing survey dependent
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - WMS/WFS services
- **URL:** http://www.onegeology.org/
- **Quality:** Good - Access to Arabian Peninsula bedrock geology including Kuwait[45][46][47]

## 10. CLIMATE / WEATHER

### **Kuwait Meteorological Department**
- **Source:** Kuwait Directorate General of Civil Aviation - Meteorological Department
- **Resolution:** Station-based observations
- **Temporal Coverage:** Historical records (1960s-present)
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time observations, daily forecasts
- **Access Type:** Partially open - historical data may require request
- **API:** Uncertain - contact department
- **URL:** https://www.met.gov.kw/
- **Quality:** Excellent - Most authoritative source for Kuwait weather data, climate statistics, temperature, humidity, precipitation[48]

### **Kuwait Automatic Weather Observation System (KAWOS)**
- **Source:** Kuwait Meteorological Services
- **Resolution:** Multiple station network
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2008-present
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time
- **Access Type:** Restricted - Government/research access
- **API:** Uncertain
- **URL:** Contact Kuwait Met Department
- **Quality:** Excellent - High-quality rain gauge and weather station data for semi-arid conditions[49]

### **ERA5 Reanalysis**
- **Source:** European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF)
- **Resolution:** 0.25° × 0.25° (~25-30 km)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1940-present (hourly data)
- **Update Frequency:** Daily updates with 5-day lag
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** Yes - Copernicus Climate Data Store API
- **URL:** https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/
- **Quality:** Excellent - Strong predictive capability for Kuwait (R~0.6-0.9), ~3% median bias for precipitation, validated against KAWOS data, superior to NCEP reanalysis[50][51][49]

### **WorldClim**
- **Source:** WorldClim / University of California
- **Resolution:** 30 arc-seconds (~1 km) to 2.5 arc-minutes
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1970-2000 baseline, future projections available
- **Update Frequency:** Periodic major updates
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** No - Download only
- **URL:** https://www.worldclim.org/
- **Quality:** Very good - Long-term climate normals for temperature, precipitation, suitable for pipeline design climate parameters

## 12. POPULATION / DEMOGRAPHICS

### **Kuwait Public Authority for Civil Information (PACI)**
- **Source:** PACI / Kuwait Central Statistics Bureau
- **Resolution:** Governorate and district level
- **Temporal Coverage:** Most recent official census 2005, current population estimates
- **Update Frequency:** Irregular census, annual estimates
- **Access Type:** Restricted/Requires request
- **API:** No public API
- **URL:** Contact PACI or Kuwait Central Statistics Bureau
- **Quality:** Excellent - Official population statistics, 4.82 million total (2024), 1.53 million Kuwaiti citizens, 3.29 million expatriates[56][57]

### **WorldPop Kuwait**
- **Source:** WorldPop / University of Southampton
- **Resolution:** 100 meters (3 arc-seconds)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2000-2020 (annual datasets)
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access Type:** Open/Free
- **API:** No - Download only
- **URL:** https://hub.worldpop.org/ and https://data.worldpop.org/
- **Quality:** Very good - UN-adjusted population density grids, useful for identifying populated areas for pipeline routing avoidance[58][59]

### **LandScan Global Population**
- **Source:** Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL)
- **Resolution:** 1 km (30 arc-seconds)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Annual datasets
- **Update Frequency:** Annual
- **Access Type:** Free for US government and academic use, commercial licensing available
- **API:** No
- **URL:** https://landscan.ornl.gov/
- **Quality:** Excellent - Ambient (24-hour average) population distribution, integrates nighttime lights and infrastructure data, well-suited for Kuwait's energy-rich context[60]

### **Esri Demographics for Kuwait**
- **Source:** Esri / Michael Bauer Research GmbH
- **Resolution:** Administrative unit aggregation
- **Temporal Coverage:** Current
- **Update Frequency:** Regular updates
- **Access Type:** Commercial - Esri subscription required
- **API:** Yes - ArcGIS Living Atlas
- **URL:** ArcGIS Living Atlas
- **Quality:** Good - Standard demographic variables for business and planning applications[61]
